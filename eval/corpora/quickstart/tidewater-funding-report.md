# Tidewater Institute Funding Report 2025

The Tidewater Institute received its 2025 funding from three sources. The Coastal Trust funded the Kestrel project, which brought the Kestrel spectrograph to Harbor Light Observatory. A regional education fund paid for the school visits that the institute hosts each spring. Membership fees covered the cost of new books for the library.

The Tidewater Institute ended the year with a small surplus. Its board meets every quarter in the town of Little Weir. The next annual report is due in the spring.
