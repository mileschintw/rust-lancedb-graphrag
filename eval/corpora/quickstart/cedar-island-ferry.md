# Cedar Island Ferry Timetable

The Cedar Island ferry leaves the mainland dock at 8:00 each morning and returns from the island at 4:30 in the afternoon. In summer a second sailing leaves at noon. The crossing takes twenty-five minutes.

Tickets are sold at the dock. Bicycles travel free, and cars must be booked a day ahead. The ferry does not run during storms. The island has one cafe, near the ferry landing, which opens when the first boat arrives.
