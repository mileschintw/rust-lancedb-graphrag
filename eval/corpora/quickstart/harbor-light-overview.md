# Harbor Light Observatory: Overview

Harbor Light Observatory stands on the northern shore of Cedar Island. The Tidewater Institute runs Harbor Light Observatory as its only field station. Marisol Vega directs Harbor Light Observatory. Marisol Vega joined the Tidewater Institute in 2019 and became the director of Harbor Light Observatory in 2022.

The observatory has one dome, a small library and a workshop. Visitors reach Cedar Island by ferry. The Kestrel spectrograph is the main instrument at Harbor Light Observatory. Staff at Harbor Light Observatory use the Kestrel spectrograph on clear nights to measure the light of nearby stars.
