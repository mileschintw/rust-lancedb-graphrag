# Installing the Kestrel Spectrograph

Alder Optics built the Kestrel spectrograph. The Kestrel spectrograph was installed at Harbor Light Observatory in March 2025. Devon Okafor was the lead engineer for the installation.

Devon Okafor and a team of four technicians mounted the Kestrel spectrograph on the main telescope of Harbor Light Observatory over two weeks. They aligned the optics, calibrated the detector and tested the Kestrel spectrograph against a known lamp. Alder Optics shipped the Kestrel spectrograph in three crates. The installation finished on schedule, and Harbor Light Observatory recorded first light the following week.
