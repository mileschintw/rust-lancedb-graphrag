# Alder Optics Company Profile

Alder Optics is based in Port Marlow. Imani Reyes is the chief executive of Alder Optics. Alder Optics was founded in 2011 and makes precision lenses, mirrors and spectrographs for research telescopes.

The company employs about sixty people in Port Marlow. Alder Optics built the Kestrel spectrograph for Harbor Light Observatory, which is the largest order in the history of Alder Optics. Imani Reyes says Alder Optics plans to open a second workshop in Port Marlow next year.
