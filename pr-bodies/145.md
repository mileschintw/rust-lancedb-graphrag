  - Its LF-normalised sha256 `0447b65e…b089` is in `twowiki.toml › [d243_split]`.
  - A test rebuilds the split from its own IDs and seed.
- **Writer pre-registration (step 3).**
  - `thresholds.WriterPreRegistration` (commit 58e5632b) is the new class.
  - `PREREGISTRATION_S3_WRITERS` (commit 0692c2f8, the D-73 freeze, with nothing else in it) pins the passages file sha256. Each passage is one unit, `title\ntext` (HippoRAG 2's form), with no chunking and no length floor.
