## What

`lancet-eval run`, `lancet-eval run-external` and `lancet-llamaindex export` now print a D-258 cost estimate before their first paid request. The estimate never refuses. The export also runs the same split gates (`run.require_split_gates`: D-73, split hash, role) that `run` and `run-external` already run, whenever its corpus has a `[split]`.

## Why

- D-258 says "A cost estimate is printed before a submit or a drive and never refuses". `batch submit` printed one (#53), but no drive command did, so Confirm and S3 had no estimate.
- The export was the only paid held-out path with no gate. Run on its own, or rerun into another directory, it read held-out questions ungated.

## How

- New `eval/src/lancet_eval/drive_estimate.py`. It computes questions x arms x a per-question figure from `measure.py`'s constants; `measure.py` itself is unchanged.
  - Engine arm: generation at the 8192/2048-token ceiling (`failed_generation_attempt_usd`), plus query embedding on an arm that embeds, plus `RERANK_UNREPORTED_CALL_CEILING_USD` on an arm with the rerank lever.
  - External or reader arm: generation only.
  - When the run has a retry budget, the line also states the bound if every unit used all its retries.
  - The export figure adds document indexing, approximated from the document text at 4 characters per token, to a per-question query embedding plus one rerank call.
- `run_benchmark` prints the estimate after arm selection and before `load_settings`/`drive`. `run_external_command` prints it before `run_external`, with `EXTERNAL_RETRIES`. A label that is not an external arm gets no estimate, because `run_external` refuses it.
- `lancet-llamaindex export` loads the corpus config when its TOML exists, runs `require_split_gates` on the questions it will read (a `--questions` override included), and prints the estimate to stderr after `load_documents` and before `Pipeline.build`. `main` now reports a gate's `ValueError` (`PreregistrationError` included) as exit 1.
- The optional held-out run-directory guard from #78 is not added: no held-out ledger, no new flag.
