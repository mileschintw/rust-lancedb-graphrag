`score` and `compare-levers` now handle an S2 run whose arms differ only by reader (D-257). `compare-levers` adds a `reader_comparison` block, and a matching "Readers" section to `lever-comparison.md`, whenever the pre-registered arms include reader arms. For each replayed base arm the block holds:

- **Per reader:** `answer_usable` on G, with the count of excluded G questions. Also the cost per answered question: sync list price × the reader's tokens over its ok G records, divided by its usable answers.
  - Prices come from the pre-registration's `ReaderDeclaration`.
  - The carried deepseek arm is priced at `measure.GENERATION_*_PRICE_PER_1M`, and the row says which source it used.
  - A thinking-on reader is costed with its reasoning tokens, which sit inside `completion_tokens`.
- **Batch spend:** the actual batch spend is reported apart. It is read from the records' usage at `BATCH_PRICE_FACTOR` (0.5) × the declared price. An arm with no batch record yet reports `"none"`, so #53 can land after this.
- **Pairs:** paired `answer_usable` differences between every pair of readers on G. Each has an exact sign-flip p, labelled as reported but not used (direction-finding, D-252).
- **Null guards:** the null guard of each reader against every other reader.

D-252's S2 rule and D-245 are applied by hand from these figures.

Pricing fails closed:

- `measure.compute_spend` prices reader-arm records at the batch rate. It refuses (`ReaderPriceError`) a reader with no declared price, and a record whose `reasoning_tokens` exceed its `completion_tokens`.
- An errored reader record, which has no replay block, costs 0.
- `compare-levers` turns both refusals into `LeverComparisonError`, and `score` turns them into `ScoreError`.

Reader arms replay their base arm's retrieval, so they are left out of every ranking metric:

- in `score`: paper Hits@4, Hits@10, MRR and MAP, evidence Hits@4, and the D-122 script-faithful lines;
- in `compare-levers`: chunk-ID metrics and evidence Hits@4.

A run with no reader arm produces the same output as before.

For #53: every caller of `compute_spend` that may see reader-arm records must pass `readers=preregistration.readers_of(prereg)`. A reader record's `completion_tokens` must include its reasoning tokens.
