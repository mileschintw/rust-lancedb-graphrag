## Summary

Implements the owner's two S2 design decisions:

- **#123, D-274.** Every S2 arm answers each question 5 times, and each question is scored by the mean of its 5 answers.
- **#127, D-275.** The S2 reader rule now picks the cheapest eligible reader unless a dearer one is significantly better after Holm.

The coordinator approved widening the scope (option 1): a unit is now (question, arm, replicate) across the journal, batch, replay, provenance, score and compare paths. Journals written before this change have no `replicate` field and read as replicate 1. Six committed runs score and compare byte-identically with and without this change (see Testing).

**Replicates (D-274)**

- `RunRecord.replicate` defaults to 1 and is left out of the JSON at 1. `deduplicate_by_arm` keys on (question, arm, replicate).
- `batch submit` sends K answers per question on a reader arm, each its own request with `custom_id` `<qid>_r<k>`. The manifest records each request's question and replicate, and `fetch` renders each replicate in its own call. A reader arm's export row is appended once per question.
- On the carried deepseek arm, `batch submit` sends replicates 2 to K. Each is the engine's `pack` body, unrewritten, through an OpenRouter batch.
  - The body must carry D-271's pin, with `config.toml`'s values: one `order` entry, `allow_fallbacks: false`, the same `max_price`.
  - Its records are replay records with their own clauses: the body hash is the `pack` body's, nothing is rewritten, the model is the body's, `is_byok` is false, the provider is the pinned one, reasoning is off and reports 0 tokens.
  - Without replicates, this arm is refused as before.
- `score` runs every replicate through the replay clauses and the provenance scan. `report.json` reads replicate 1.
- `compare-levers`:
  - A question's value is its replicate mean. A question enters a comparison only when all K answers of both arms are ok, and the reader rows use the same rule.
  - The test is the exact two-sided sign-flip permutation on the per-question mean differences. Each difference times K is an integer, so every sign vector is counted exactly (`stats.exact_signflip_p_integer`), with no seed.
  - A paired t-test sits beside it as `paired_t_p`, a cross-check only.
  - The CI is the existing bootstrap over questions.
  - The null guard pairs replicate k with replicate k, so its counts are K times the questions'.
  - At K = 1 every one of these paths is the old code.
- `thresholds.ReplayStagePreRegistration` gains `replicates`, and `REPLICATE_TEST` names the new test. A constant with replicates above 1 and any other test is refused. No `PREREGISTRATION_S2` literal is added (D-73). The draft in EXPERIMENT-S2.md now has `replicates=5`.

**Reader rule (D-275)**

The starting pick is the eligible reader with the lowest cost per answered question, over all 5 answers. It wins unless a dearer eligible reader is significantly better: Holm runs once over every pair of candidates, and the pick steps up to the cheapest reader whose pair with the current pick Holm rejects in that reader's favour. The step-up and the Holm family are this lane's reading of the owner's words, and PLAN.md says so. EXPERIMENT-S2 › Smallest detectable effect states about 8.4 points at K = 5 and n = 90.
