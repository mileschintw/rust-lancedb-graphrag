## Summary

Pins the engine's deepseek generation and graph extraction to Wafer's US endpoint with a price cap (D-271, amends
D-191). Also fixes the extraction truncation that cost the S1 seed about 18% of its chunks (D-272). This must merge
before S1 step 4.2; the S1 store is rebuilt after it.

**Provider pin (D-271).** Both requests now send `provider: {order: ["wafer/us"], allow_fallbacks: false, max_price:
{prompt: 0.07, completion: 0.45}}`. Generation keeps D-96's `require_parameters: true` beside it. Extraction sends
the pin alone and still sends no `provider` key when nothing is pinned. Before this change, extraction sent no pin at
all, and generation was pinned to `sail-research` with no variant. S0's generation ran on `sail-research`; D-271
records that. The `max_price` syntax ($ per million tokens, an object of `prompt` / `completion`) was checked on
2026-10-31 against https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/provider-selection.

**Extraction cap and timeout (D-272).** The extraction request's `max_completion_tokens` came from a hard-coded `768`
in `main.rs`. It did not come from `max_output_tokens = 2048`. Answers were cut mid-JSON and failed with "EOF while
parsing". Extraction now sends its own `EXTRACTION_MAX_COMPLETION_TOKENS = 16384`, which fits a schema-maximal answer
(about 11,900 estimated tokens) with a 25% margin. It also gets its own `EXTRACTION_TIMEOUT = 120 s`, so long answers
don't turn into timeouts. Generation keeps 2048 tokens and 30 s.

- `engine/src/generation/openrouter.rs`: new `MaxPrice` and `ProviderPin` types. `ProviderPreferences` flattens the
  pin beside `require_parameters`. `with_max_price` was added to the config.
- `engine/src/graph/extraction.rs`: the request carries the pin. Adds `EXTRACTION_MAX_COMPLETION_TOKENS` and
  `EXTRACTION_TIMEOUT`.
- `engine/src/main.rs`: the extraction config gets the provider order, the price cap and the extraction timeout. The
  generation config gets the price cap.
- `engine/src/bin/generate_from_context.rs`: the pack path adds the price cap, so its body still equals the live body.
- `engine/src/config.rs`: new `generation_max_price_prompt` / `generation_max_price_completion` keys (optional,
  finite and above 0, not environment-overridable).
- `config/config.toml`, `config/config.example.toml`: pin `["wafer/us"]` with caps of 0.07 / 0.45.
- `experiments/v1.1/PLAN.md`: D-271 and D-272.

Nothing in `eval/` pins `sail-research`: provenance and scoring read the order from `config/config.toml`.
`eval/tests/fixtures/reader_request/pack_body.json` still shows the old `sail-research` provider. It is a test
fixture whose `provider` is replaced by `rewrite_pack_body`, so it was left as is.
