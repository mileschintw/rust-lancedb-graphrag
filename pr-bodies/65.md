+        no source for that store / bad prefix  -> fail closed, naming question/arm

- `export_context` and `write_context_export` take one `CheckpointSource` (as before) or a map of store name to source (`StoreSources`).
- `store_checkpoint_sources(settings)` keeps `settings` unchanged for the store they target, and points every other store at its own `search_path` on the same database.
- The CLI form stays `lancet-eval export-context --run <dir>`, with no `--store`: one command rebuilds a two-store export.
