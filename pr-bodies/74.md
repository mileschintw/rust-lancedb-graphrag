### Run guide (#75)

The *Rules* section gains **Count after each drive**. After steps 3.2, 3.5, 4.2 and 4.4 the worker:

- reads the drive's output for `Drive stopped early`, which a tripwire stop (D-262) or a cap stop prints while still exiting 0;
- counts the journal's records per arm and outcome, keeping the later record of each question and arm, the way `score` reads the journal;
- prints every `error` record.

Each drive step names the count every arm must reach: 351 for S0, 100 for S1. On `Drive stopped early` or a short count, the worker stops and asks the user. Re-driving after a tripwire halt stays the user's decision (D-174). Step 3.5 says that while `llamaindex` has errors, the worker may ask for a yes to rerun `run-external`, at most twice.

### `run-external` resume (#75)

`journal.load_done` counts an error record as done, so before this change a transient reader failure was final for the external arm, while `run` retries the Lancet arm twice inside the drive (`--retries 2`). Now a resume also re-reads each question whose latest external record is an error, until that question has had `1 + EXTERNAL_RETRIES` (3) attempts. The new record goes after the error record. Every reader S0 uses already keeps the later record (`pairing.deduplicate_by_arm`), and `compute_spend` still charges every attempt toward the cap. `journal.py` and `cli.py` are unchanged, and no flag was added.
