## Summary

`answer_usable` rewards a guess on yes/no questions: a constant Yes scored about 0.66 in S2. This PR adds figures
that read the score apart, as reported secondaries only. It also records the owner's accepted S3 design principles
as D-281 to D-289.

- **Guessing-adjusted figure: balanced accuracy** (D-286). It is the mean of `answer_usable` over gold-Yes and over
  gold-No questions. Any constant answer scores 0.5 whatever the gold mix, and abstaining on every question scores 0.
  The excess over the better constant answer was rejected because it moves with each set's Yes share. The better
  constant's share is reported next to it for context.
- **Evidence split:** complete when every gold fact is in the reader's context, else partial. It reuses #143's
  `evidence_split.evidence_complete` and `is_yes_no`. A reader arm reads its base arm's export row. Without a
  context export, the split is left out.
- **Unchanged:** no pre-registration constant and no decisional metric. `PREREGISTRATION_S0` to `S2X` keep
  `answer_usable`, and `PREREGISTRATION_S3_WRITERS` names no metric.
- **Replicates:** `score` splits one value per question (the replicate mean). `compare-levers` counts every
  replicate's answer, as its reader rows already do.
