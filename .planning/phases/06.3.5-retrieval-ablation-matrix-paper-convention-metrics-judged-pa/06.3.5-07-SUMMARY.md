## Accomplishments

- `paper_question_scores(ranked, gold, matches)` follows the official function exactly: window of 10, any-gold-unit relevance, `rr = 1/first`, and the script's non-standard `ap` (new facts first found at a rank over that rank, divided by `min(len(gold), 10)`). It is generic over the matcher: `id_matcher` with `frozenset` units for the ID form, `text_matcher` (strips spaces and newlines on both sides, case-sensitive) for the text form.
- Golden fixture (247 cases, 192 KB): the official file was fetched to a scratch directory (never the repo), loaded with `importlib`, and called per question. Cases cover 240 seeded random trials (with embedded newlines, ranks 1-14), the negative control (official AP 0.75 vs textbook 1.0), an empty gold unit, a hit only at rank 11, first hits at ranks 4, 5 and 10, and an empty ranking. Header: repo, commit c1c1287aa60a94acf9c4d20c891c9cd611a0f6e8, file, sha256, generator, seed. Regenerating from the same official file is byte-identical.
- `load_gold_chunk_sets` reads the real 1185-row table into 447 questions with `split_across_chunks` rows as empty sets, ordered by `evidence_index`, failing closed on an unknown state, an `in_chunk` row without IDs or a duplicate `(question, index)`.
- `provenance.py`: one coded failure per broken clause (a) mode echo, (b) ranking present and bounded, (c) prefix equals the final list, (d) per-arm rank shape, (e) GRAPH_ABLATION, (f) drive-2 config, (g) corroboration only. `is_ok` = D-34 usable and no failure in a-f. `ZERO_TOLERANCE_CODES` = a, b, c, d, f.
- D-104 retro: the committed `06.3.5-RETRO-PAPER-HITS4.md` starts with `dev-only, paper-convention @4` and reports hybrid (stored graph-off) 68/90 = 0.7556 (Wilson [0.658, 0.833]) and hybrid+graph (stored graph-on) 65/89 = 0.7303 (Wilson [0.630, 0.812]). It reports nothing beyond rank 4 and says ranks 9-10 are absent from the journal.
