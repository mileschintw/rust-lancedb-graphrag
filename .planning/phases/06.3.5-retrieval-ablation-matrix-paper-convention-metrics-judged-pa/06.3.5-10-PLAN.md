<objective>
Produce the deterministic half of the four-arm run of record through the unchanged fail-closed path. That is every
per-arm retrieval, answer, abstention, latency and cost number, on P4 and on the script-faithful line, as numeric
`report.json` dimensions. `score` refuses to report a drive whose arm provenance is broken. The tool that checks the
ID matching rule against the official text rule is built here.

ROADMAP goal (quoted verbatim, not reframed; see 06.3.5-01). SC-7: "per-arm retrieval, answer and judged metrics with
Wilson or bootstrap CIs, paired deltas of every arm against `hybrid` ... latency and prompt-token cost per arm". The
judged metrics come in 06.3.5-12, and the paired deltas and Holm in 06.3.5-13.

Purpose: D-126 places the four-arm numbers in `report.json` without a schema change. AI-SPEC §6 tightens provenance for
the paid drive: one deterministic-switch failure means the plumbing is wrong for an unknown share of records.

Output: the four-arm dimensions in `score`, the provenance refusal, and `paper_text_crosscheck.py`.
</objective>
