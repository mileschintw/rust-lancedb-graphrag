## Accomplishments

- `lancet_eval.split` holds `HeldOutSplit` (frozen, `extra="forbid"`, rejects overlap, duplicate IDs and malformed sha256), `lf_sha256`, `load_split` and `split_sha256`. One `lf_sha256` serves the generator and its tests, so a CRLF checkout cannot change a recorded hash.
- `select_heldout_split.py` regenerates the split byte-identically. It prints the facts the owner approved: dev 100 (90 G + 10 null), held-out G 308 (comparison 125, inference 105, temporal 78), held-out null 43, total 351, rehearsal pool 49 (18/22/9), `populations_sha256` `81a3cc2a...8d1`, `diag_selection_sha256` `c69d9a5d...a8d`, `questions_sample_sha256` `e15227bf...a97`, `order_seed` 42.
- Canary picks (D-124, `canary.arms.jsonl`): `mhr-0d5e238015ef` [inference], `mhr-2f3278d47eb7` [comparison], `mhr-bb3f4ad63839` [temporal]. Rehearsal picks (D-108, `questions.rehearsal.jsonl`): `mhr-325f99830534` [temporal], `mhr-6378209a1af2` [comparison], `mhr-7f86f764aaf7` [inference]. All come from the 49-question pool, one `random.Random(42)` stream, so the two sets are disjoint from each other, from dev and from held-out.
- `multihop_rag_heldout.toml` (351 questions, role `heldout`) and `multihop_rag_rehearsal.toml` (3 questions, role `rehearsal`) share the seeded index through `map_corpus = "multihop_rag"`, carry the identical judge `[models]` block, declare the four arms, `[judge] protocol = "ordered"` and `[preflight]` arm canaries with `legacy_canaries = false`.
- `.gitignore` gains `!eval/runs/*-multihop_rag_heldout/` and `!eval/runs/*-multihop_rag_rehearsal/`. `git check-ignore` exits 1 for a journal under each new run directory and 0 for a scratch directory.
