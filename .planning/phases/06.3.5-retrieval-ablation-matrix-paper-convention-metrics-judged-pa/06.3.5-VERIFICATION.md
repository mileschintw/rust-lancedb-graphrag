### The engine change versus the goal's wording

The goal states "The system under test is NOT changed in this phase (no reranker, no graph change, no prompt change), so every reading stays comparable to `eval/runs/2026-10-06-drive2-multihop_rag_diag/`." Plan 18 (`e4ae2b4e`, 2026-10-07) edited `engine/src/generation/final_answer.rs`, `generation/mod.rs`, `generation/openrouter.rs`, `workflow/nodes/generate.rs` and `workflow/mod.rs`. By the goal's letter, the system under test changed once.

What the evidence shows:

- **Scope.** The edit is a validator-level normalisation only. A cited `model_only` output whose last published line is the abstention text, and whose own trailing `Answer:` segment agrees, is read as `retrieval` basis. Uncited and substantive `model_only` outputs stay rejected. The prompt, config, proto, gateway and the default-request golden are untouched (commit message and Plan 18 SUMMARY). Retrieval and ranking run before generation, so retrieval metrics (Hits@k, MRR, coverage) are unaffected by construction.
- **Cause predates the phase.** STATE.md line 41 records that the same rejection class appeared in drive 2 (two records, corr ids `c5384336...`, `2fb73c03...`), which is the 06.3.4.1 run of record's `model_only_unsupported` count of 2. So the old engine had the same defect on the reference run; the fix converts such records from errors to normal abstentions.
- **Disclosure.** `BASIS_RECONCILED` notice on every affected record. I counted 118 records carrying it in `journal.jsonl` (bm25-only 40, dense-only 27, hybrid 26, hybrid+graph 25), matching `diagnostic/basis_normalisation.json`. Disclosed in `06.3.5-DRIVE.md` (preflight attempt 1 failure, `model_only_rejected` 0, per-arm table), in the run of record's "Engine change disclosed (06.3.5-18)" bullet, and in the Plan 18 SUMMARY.
- **Timing and freeze.** The change landed before the held-out drive, on canary and dev evidence; `bb4bf0a0` re-rehearsal passed its provenance gate; no source change between that commit and the drive (empty `git diff`). D-106/D-49 are intact: nothing was tuned after a held-out reading.

How I weigh it: the goal's intent is preserved for the four arms, since all four ran on the same engine, so the ablation deltas are internally sound, and the retrieval half of the comparison to drive 2 is untouched. The goal's letter is not preserved, and comparability to drive 2 is weaker on the answer and error-rate side: 118 of 1,404 held-out records (8.4%) carried the mislabel, versus 2 of 200 in drive 2, so without the fix a material share of held-out records would have been errors rather than abstentions. That is a real difference in behaviour on the answer path, not a cosmetic one. It was owner-authorised, disclosed per record and per arm, and made before any held-out reading. I regard it as a disclosed, defensible deviation and not a failed truth, but whether it satisfies the goal wording is the owner's call, so G-1 is UNCERTAIN rather than VERIFIED. Residual weakness: the ROADMAP goal text is not amended, and the run of record does not say in a sentence that "comparable to drive 2" holds for retrieval but is qualified for abstention and error counts (Warning W-1).
