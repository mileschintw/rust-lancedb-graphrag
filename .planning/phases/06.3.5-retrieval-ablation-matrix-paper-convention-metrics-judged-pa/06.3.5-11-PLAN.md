Build the first half of the ordered judged pass, offline and tested. A cache-only judge stage judges every
judgeable held-out record of all four arms and shows nothing but counts (D-112, D-120 step 1). An emit step draws
the blinded 20-item calibration slice, commit-reveal keyed, before any judged number exists (D-113, D-120 step 2).

ROADMAP goal (quoted verbatim, not reframed; see 06.3.5-01). SC-6: "The judged dimensions (`answer_faithfulness`,
`answer_groundedness`) run on the held-out records of all arms with cached verdicts, and a human calibration slice of
at least 20 items is scored by the project owner".

Purpose: today one `score_run` call judges, emits the worksheet and rewrites `report.json` with judged aggregates, and
only the primary arm is judged. D-113's ordering, where the owner scores before any judged aggregate is shown, cannot
hold that way. The cut is enforced in code. The paid judge run is 06.3.5-16, and the judged aggregates are 06.3.5-12.

Output: `judge_stage.py`, `calibration.py` (draw and emit), and the `judge` and `calibration emit` commands.
</objective>
