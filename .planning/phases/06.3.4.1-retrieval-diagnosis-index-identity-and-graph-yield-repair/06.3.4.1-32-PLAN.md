<objective>
Stop the unpark gates from scoring a partial journal. This closes UAT gap G-06.3.4.1-3a (06.3.4.1-REVIEW.md
CR-02 and WR-04's coverage disclosure). `main` measures completeness once, and every reading MISSes on an
incomplete journal. SC-1 requires a complete journal. SC-3, SC-4 and SC-5 MISS when their population falls
below a committed coverage floor against the sample-scoped G and V denominators.

ROADMAP goal (verbatim, not reframed as a user story; the phase is non-MVP): "Separate seeding/index
drift vs vector/hybrid retrieval vs graph architecture; restore a working vector baseline; then make
graph-on produce a measurable paired effect."

Purpose:
- The gate table is how Phase 6.4 decides to unpark. Today a drive halted at 20% (40/200 units), or one with
  just two records, reads all-PASS (debug session `unpark-gates-score-partial-journal.md`). This breaches the
  plan-01 test-tier prohibition against presenting a partial drive as a complete scored run, so far in latent form.
- Completeness alone does not cover a complete journal whose G or V records error out, because
  `load_done` counts error records as done. The coverage floor closes that second path.

Output: a fail-closed `main`, the SC-1 predicate, coverage clauses with one new pre-registered literal, and
real-data tests that show the old all-PASS truncations now MISS and the recorded verdicts unchanged.
</objective>
