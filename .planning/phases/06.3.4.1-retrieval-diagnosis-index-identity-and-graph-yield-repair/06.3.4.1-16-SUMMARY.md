## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` above. Two points of method:

- **The bound on `graph_seed_document_ids` was measured, not assumed.** Plan 14 flagged the list as unbounded for hub seeds (`Google` has 543 edges). The seed probe records each seed's source documents, so the per-question union was computed from it: at most 88 IDs, because a question has at most `max_seeds` 6 seeds and a seed's chunks sit in a bounded number of documents (47 at most on the probe). A cap would also cost the figure the field exists for, so none was added. If a much larger corpus ever makes the list large, the right response is a count plus a membership flag computed in the engine, which needs the gold document and so belongs to a harness-side change, not a silent truncation.
- **The mapping guard scans source text.** A harness field the client reads and a drive site forgets to copy is dropped without an error, because the journal model defaults it. A field-set equality test between the two models plus a scan of the three copy sites turns that into a failing test.

### Departures from the plan's text

**4. [Interface] A separate DTO for retrieved chunks.** The plan said to add `GraphBoosted` "to the retrieved-chunk DTO in `dto.go`"; there was none, because `StructuredCitationDTO` serves both `structured_citations` and `snapshot.retrieved_chunks`. Adding the key there would have given citations a ten-key shape (breaking the nine-key assertions in `main_test.go`) and let a cited chunk claim a flag it was never given. `RetrievedChunkDTO` embeds the shared DTO (so existing field access still compiles) and adds the always-present flag.

**5. [Interface] One shared Python model.** The plan said "the retrieved-chunk models in both" `client.py` and `journal.py`. The journal has no model of its own: `journal.RunRecord` imports `client.StructuredCitation`, so the field is one edit. As a result a journalled citation also gets `"graph_boosted": null`, which no reader treats as a flag.

**6. [Test strength] The fake port reports 3 capped paths, not 0.** The plan's behaviour example says "0 capped". With a nonzero value a field copied from the wrong source cannot pass by being zero. A graph-off workflow, and the production query (whose real count is 0), cover zero.

[Addition] Tests the plan did not list.** A failure-path workflow, seeds with no path, a nil-versus-empty list, the cited-chunk flag, the next-free-tag pin, the older-message decode, the span source scan, a production-ports query over a temp store, the absent-versus-zero harness case and the field-set parity guard.

**8. [Tooling] RED evidence by hand** (see RED evidence), including the corrected regex in the GREEN commit.

**9.
