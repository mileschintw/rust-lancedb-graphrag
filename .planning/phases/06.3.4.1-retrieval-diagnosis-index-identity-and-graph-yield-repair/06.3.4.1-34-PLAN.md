## Flagged Assumptions

- **Legacy carve-out (planner decision, disclosed).** Read literally, the owner's "readers MISS a journal
  lacking the marker" would turn drives 1, 1b and 2 to all-MISS on any re-read, because they predate the
  marker. The orchestrator's constraint says their verdicts stay unchanged. The two are reconciled the way
  the owner scoped the coverage floor ("governs drives started after its commit"): the marker rule governs
  every journal except the three recorded gate drives. Those are named in a closed registry keyed on their
  own header content, each with its `--retries 0` console evidence (`drive-console.txt`: `retries=0` for
  all three, checked at planning time). Every other pre-marker journal MISSes, so no time window and no
  file-hash key (which a CRLF checkout would change) can let an unmarked journal through. Re-reading the
  recorded drives under the new reader is a labelled disclosure; `gates-drive*.{md,json}` are not rewritten.
- **First attempt, not every attempt.** SC-2 and D-69 count each work unit's first attempt once, the reading
  D-67 defines ("every timeout appears once"). Counting every attempt would weight a unit by how often it
  was retried. Retried journals MISS in a gate anyway, so this choice matters for direct evaluator calls.
- **Out of scope:** the 06.3.4 `staged-gate` reader (`gate.py`, `lancet-eval staged-gate`). It is not among
  the gap's artifacts, and its only recorded run (2026-09-09) is superseded (D-88). It stays retry-blind and
  is disclosed here, not changed.
