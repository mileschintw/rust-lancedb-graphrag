<objective>
Make graph-on change what is retrieved, not only what is appended to the prompt (D-76, D-81): inject the
graph's candidate chunks into RetrieveHybrid's fusion as a third RRF list with Graph provenance, and flag
graph-boosted chunks so the composition change can be measured.

Purpose: SC-5 requires a paired effect a reader can see. RESEARCH §F.4 recommends RRF injection over a
score boost because a score boost can only reorder chunks that dense and BM25 already found.

Output: the fusion change, the graph-chunk fetch, per-chunk provenance and the new setting, with a
graph-off invariance test.
</objective>
