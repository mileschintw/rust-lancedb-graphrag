<objective>
Make harness retries leave a durable trace and stop gate-stage drives from using them. This is the drive
side of UAT gap G-06.3.4.1-2, which folds in 06.3.4.1-REVIEW.md CR-03. Every superseded attempt is kept on
the journaled record, spend charges each attempt, and a gate-stage drive declares itself in the journal
header and refuses retries. The gate readers that act on this marker are 06.3.4.1-34.

ROADMAP goal (verbatim, not reframed as a user story; the phase is non-MVP): "Separate seeding/index
drift vs vector/hybrid retrieval vs graph architecture; restore a working vector baseline; then make
graph-on produce a measurable paired effect."

Purpose:
- D-67 says every timeout appears once in SC-2's error-mode count. Today `drive_one` keeps only the final
  attempt, and `lancet-eval run` defaults to `--retries 2`. A timeout that succeeds on retry vanishes, and
  SC-2 flips from MISS to PASS (debug repro). The billed attempt is also missing from `compute_spend`, so
  the stage cap undercounts.
- The run of record was clean only because the operator typed `--retries 0`. That control was procedural.
  The 06.3.4 D-69 baseline, recorded under `--retries 2`, shows the gap has already bent one figure.

Output: the attempt schema and its recording, per-attempt spend, and the drive-time gate-stage marker with
its refusals.
</objective>
