## Flagged Assumptions

- **The policy is reworded by appending, not by rewriting (reading of D-95).** D-95 says the policy "is reworded for the new field" while keeping every guard sentence, the citation contract, the injection guard and the F-1 clause, and the goldens are "extended, never deleted". `PRE_F1_POLICY` pins the four D-71 sentences verbatim, and the F-1 test pins F-1 verbatim. Rewording any of them would mean editing a golden. So every existing sentence stays byte-identical, one D-95 sentence is appended, and new goldens pin it. The model still writes its own `Answer:` line in `answer`, as D-71 asks, and the engine replaces that trailing segment with the rendered one (next bullet).
- **Decided here: the trailing model-written `Answer:` segment is stripped when a line renders.** The strip applies only to the prose's last line: the last case-sensitive `Answer:` there, line-start or inline, with no square bracket in the segment, and only when prose would remain. A bracketed segment is kept, because removing it could drop a marker counted in the citations. Two Answer lines are then visible, and the rendered one is still the last line-start match. So the visible text normally carries exactly one final line, and extraction never depends on the strip.
- **Empty field means text identical to today (reading of "scores as `final_answer_missing`, as today").** A `None`, empty or whitespace-only field, after sanitising, renders nothing and strips nothing. The answer is exactly the model's, and the committed extractor scores it as it would have before D-95. The record is `final_answer_missing` unless the model's own prose carries a line-start `Answer:`. Forcing a miss by deleting a model-written line would change today's behaviour, which D-95's "as today" rules out.
- **Whole-field fit rule.** A field that does not fit whole within `MAX_ANSWER_CHARS` renders no line, rather than a truncated one, so no partial short answer is ever scored.
