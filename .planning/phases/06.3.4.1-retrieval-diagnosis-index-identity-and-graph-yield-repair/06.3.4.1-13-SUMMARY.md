The orchestrator analysis the user decided on, with its method and table, is in SEED-PROBE.md section 9. In short: on the 30 G questions with no path, the fallback would give 4 questions any gold chunk (1 a new gold chunk, 6 a new chunk from a gold document missing from the dense top-4, 10 only non-gold documents); on the 60 with a path both options are identical. Chunk level only, not answer quality, n = 30.
