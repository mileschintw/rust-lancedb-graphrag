## 4. D-59 candidate list and disposition

**Root cause found and fixed before this probe ran (Rule 1 bug, commit `fb3691b8`):**
`classify_evidence_item`'s adjacent-window `split_across_chunks` check
(`engine/src/bin/inspect_lancedb.rs`) tested two adjacent chunks by naive space-joining them
(`format!("{content_a} {content_b}")`). Adjacent chunks share a `DEFAULT_CHUNK_OVERLAP`-sized
(50-char) region of identical text; the naive join duplicates that region and inserts an
artificial space that never existed in the source document. Any fact whose true span starts
before the overlap and ends beyond it could therefore never match, and was misclassified
`absent` instead of `split_across_chunks`. Task 1's first post-reconcile probe run (before the
fix) reported 49 evidence items (18 documents) as `absent`; the orchestrator verified all 49 are
in fact substrings of the true overlap-merged span, root-caused the bug, and the user directed
the fix. After the fix (`merge_overlapping_chunks`, removes the duplicated overlap before
concatenating), the same 49 items across the same 18 documents are correctly `split_across_chunks`,
and **0 items remain `absent`** on the reconciled store.
