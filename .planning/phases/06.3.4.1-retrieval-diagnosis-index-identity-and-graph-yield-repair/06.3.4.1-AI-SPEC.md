## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Hybrid — multi-hop RAG / GraphRAG system under test (Rust engine: LanceDB hybrid retrieval +
entity/edge graph, OpenRouter generation) **plus** its Python `lancet-eval` evaluation harness.

**Description:**
06.3.4's halted drive left both arms near floor (graph-off EM 0.0000, Token-F1 0.0223, Recall@4 0.3613,
Precision@4 0.2150; graph presence 9.8%). This phase splits the hypotheses instead of assuming the cause, in
the ROADMAP's fixed layer order: (1) consume the fail-closed/`partial` path, (2) index identity — surgical
reconcile to the 346-doc `document_map.json` set with a hard preflight gate, (3) `RetrieveHybrid` health — find and
fix the in-process accumulation that makes latency grow linearly per record, then re-derive budgets, (4) vector
baseline — an additive `Answer: <short>` final-answer line in the production prompt plus a lenient deterministic
metric, judged on the gold-in-index subset, (5) graph repair — mention-based multi-entity seeding, seed-to-seed
paths (≤2 hops), and a chunk boost that visibly changes retrieval composition. "Good" is a per-question yes/no
diagnostic table and a run of record whose SC-1..SC-5 gates a reviewer can trace to usable, paired, gold-in-index
records.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. **Misattributing the floor.** Crediting a gain to graph/index/latency fixes when it came from answer
   *format* (median answer 105 words vs short gold). D-74 retro-scoring on the 06.3.4 journal must separate
   the two before any paid drive.
2. **Publishing a false-complete or non-comparable number.** Scoring/reporting a halted run, bypassing the
   fail-closed path for the retro analysis, or comparing new EM/F1/presence to old runs without the D-88 marker.
3. **Latency "fixed" by workaround.** A restart, raised budget or retry masking the accumulation (D-64/D-67);
   timeouts leaking into quality denominators (06.3.1-CONTEXT D-34).
4. **Graph "effect" that is noise or tuning.** Unfiltered neighbourhood injection (06.3.4's -0.0024 F1),
   hub blow-up, or iterating toward the gate number rather than a table-justified fix (D-77, D-83, 06.3.1-CONTEXT D-49).
5. **Breaking the production answer contract.** The final-answer line raising citation-constraint rejections
   (D-69/D-71) or weakening injection guards in `engine/src/prompt.rs`; destructive reconcile deleting entities
   shared with mapped docs (D-58).

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

**Dimension: Short-answer format matching the gold's shape**
- **Good:** Model produces an explicit `Answer: <short>` line (≤10 tokens) in addition to cited explanation. Final answer scored via SQuAD-normalized exact match. Final-answer EM reported alongside unchanged EM/F1. For binary golds, extracted line is the only trustworthy score.
- **Bad:** Scoring explanation containing gold via substring (gold `no` inside "not"/"know"). Treating EM=0.0000 on 147 records as retrieval failure when whole-token containment 91/147=61.9% tells a different story. (Kamalloo et al. 2023 show lexical EM/F1 substantially underestimates correct LLM answers in open-domain QA.)
- **Stakes:** High
- **Source:** Tang & Yang (2024), *MultiHop-RAG: Benchmarking Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-Hop Queries* — https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15391. Kamalloo et al. (ACL 2023), *Evaluating Open-Domain Question Answering in the Era of Large Language Models* — https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06984.

**Dimension: Index identity as a measured precondition**
- **Good:** Hard gate compares document-ID set across PostgreSQL, LanceDB, and `document_map.json` — all three identical. Per-question check: every evidence item's gold chunk exists in LanceDB (column b = yes for all). Gold-in-index subset (chunk-level) is the denominator for quality headlines.
- **Bad:** Doc-level coverage (all gold titles in subset — true for all 447 questions, uninformative). Assuming identity without measurement. Mixed denominators.
- **Stakes:** High
- **Source:** 06.3.4.1-CONTEXT D-61–D-63; 06.3.3-STORE-BASELINE.md (baseline record template). Index drift is a known source of non-comparability across phases.

**Dimension: Multi-entity graph seeding with bridge-path cost bounding**
- **Good:** Extract multiple named entities from question (e.g. "Jonathan Smith", "CBSSports.com") via mention matching, not full-question embedding. Per-mention `name_vector` fallback allowed (no query-time LLM, D-75). Graph contribution: paths connecting seeds (≤2 hops, D-76), not unfiltered 1-hop neighbourhoods. Cost bounded by degree cap (D-77). Retrieval composition visibly changes (seed chunks + path chunks in top-k).
- **Bad:** Seed from whole-question embedding, inject full neighbourhood (-0.0024 F1 in 06.3.4). Hub nodes (degree 724) dumping entire neighbourhood without cost bounding. Uninterpretable black-box graph effect.
- **Stakes:** High
- **Source:** HippoRAG (https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14831) — personalized PageRank from query entities with node-specificity weighting. HotpotQA (arXiv:1809.09600) — bridge/comparison questions require entity chaining. MuSiQue/2Wiki practice — multiple supporting documents, explicit bridge-entity paths.

**Dimension: Latency health over the full drive**
- **Good:** `RetrieveHybrid` median latency shows no increase over ~200 records. No engine restart between first and last record. `--retries 0` ensures each timeout visible once. Per-node `duration_ms` recorded for every record. Timeout count and per-node error-mode breakdown disclosed.
- **Bad:** Latency resets after restart (hiding accumulation). `--retries 2` tripling wall-clock and burying first-attempts. Healthy slice followed by degradation. Budget derived from leaking engine.
- **Stakes:** High
- **Source:** 06.3.3-MEASUREMENT.md (flatness criterion, restart discriminator); 06.3.4-OBSERVATION-THROUGHPUT-PLATEAU.md (linear escalation, retry multiplier).

**Dimension: Separated hypotheses, paired measurements**
- **Good:** Hypotheses are tested in the fixed layer order and attributed stage by stage: the free retro-score (format only, D-74), then paid drive 1 (index + OI-02 + prompt, gates SC-2/SC-3), then paid drive 2 (graph repair, gates SC-4/SC-5). Each drive is two arms (graph-off/graph-on) over the same ~100 questions, paired within-question, with a CI and the pairing coverage stated. Per-question diagnostics: outcome, error class (D-69), seed hit, path found. Report discloses which hypothesis fixed at which stage. If gate missed after one table-justified iteration, that is disclosed (D-83).
- **Bad:** Combined metric averaging across fixes. Missing pairing/CI. Iterating toward SC-4 number, not justified fix. Halted runs scored as complete.
- **Stakes:** High
- **Source:** Voorhees & Buckley (SIGIR 2002); Sakai (SIGIR 2014); Smucker, Allan & Carterette (SIGIR 2007) — paired comparison on same topics, paired bootstrap CI. D-37–D-43.

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

1. **Format artifact read as retrieval failure.** Graph-off median answer 105 words; gold short (Yes/No/entity). EM=0.0000 while whole-token containment 91/147=61.9%. D-74 retroactive scoring separates attribution: gain on old data without backend fix reveals the floor.

2. **Substring containment inflates scoring on binary golds.** Of 106 substring vs 91 token hits on 147 records, 13 are gold `no` inside "not"/"know"/"none". For binary questions, only extracted `Answer:` line is trustworthy; explanation containment is a false-positive trap.

3. **Hub neighbourhood injection degrades specificity.** Degree-724 nodes dumping unfiltered 1-hop neighbourhoods add noise (-0.0024 F1 in 06.3.4). Without degree-capping before expansion, large components exceed budget bounds (D-77).

4. **Index/latency drift narrows or kills pairs.** Pre-drive reconcile gate (D-61) prevents identity skew. Latency leak "fixed" by restart hides in-process state; early/late records have different resource constraints (D-64/D-67).

### Entry Point Pattern

**D-74 retro-scoring on the 06.3.4 journal (read-only offline diagnostic; never calls `score`/`report`):**

```python
"""D-74: lenient gold-containment on the halted 06.3.4 journal, graph-off successes only.

Read-only. Never calls `lancet-eval score`/`report`, never writes report.json, adds no bypass
of the fail-closed path (SC-1 / T-06.3.4-19). Output label: "partial-run diagnostic, n=<N>".
"""
from pathlib import Path

from lancet_eval.corpus import load_sample_questions
from lancet_eval.journal import load_records  # skips the header line, same as resume
from lancet_eval.metrics import squad_normalize

JOURNAL = Path("eval/runs/2026-09-09-multihop_rag/journal.jsonl")


def gold_contained(gold: str, answer: str) -> bool:
    """Whole-token containment of the SQuAD-normalized gold in the normalized answer."""
    g, a = squad_normalize(gold).split(), squad_normalize(answer).split()
    return bool(g) and any(a[i : i + len(g)] == g for i in range(len(a) - len(g) + 1))


gold = {q.question_id: q for q in load_sample_questions("multihop_rag")}
rows = []
for rec in load_records(JOURNAL):
    if rec.graph_arm != "graph-off" or rec.outcome != "success" or not rec.answer:
        continue  # arms are "graph-on"/"graph-off"; D-34: usable records only
    q = gold.get(rec.question_id)
    if q is None or q.is_null:  # D-72: null_query goes to the abstention metric, not here
        continue
    rows.append((q.question_type, gold_contained(q.gold_answer, rec.answer)))

n, hit = len(rows), sum(h for _, h in rows)
print(f"partial-run diagnostic (06.3.4 journal, graph-off successes), n={n}: containment {hit}/{n}")
# There is no final-answer line in this journal (the prompt predates D-71), so final_answer_em is
# undefined here; only containment can be computed retroactively.
```

**Verified by running this code at spec time (2026-09-22): `n=147: containment 91/147` (61.9%).** CONTEXT's
"106 of 150" used **character-substring** containment over all 150 successes, including 3 null_query records.
On the 147 non-null records, substring gives 106 and whole-token gives 91. Of the 15 extra substring hits,
13 are gold `no` matching inside another word (`not`, `know`, `none`, …). **91/147 is the honest D-74
baseline.** Use it, not 106/150, when proposing the D-73 SC-3 floor and when attributing the
format-vs-fix gain.

The same `gold_contained` becomes the D-70 lenient containment metric in `eval/src/lancet_eval/metrics.py`.
Use one implementation for both, so the retro number and the drive numbers are the same metric.

### Common Pitfalls

1. **Misattributing latency reset to a fix (D-64 checkpoint).** 06.3.3 saw latency reset after engine restart (13.5s → 2.3s), suggesting in-process accumulation. But the profiling approach matters: if `variant_count` or `prompt_tokens` grow per record on a single connection, that's client-side session state; if only RetrieveHybrid slows (vector/BM25 sub-steps), suspect LanceDB table metadata caching or fragment count growth. Profile per-node before claiming root cause. **Line:** 06.3.4-CONTEXT: "median node time per 50-record slice: 5.0s → 29.0s then 100% timeouts from record ~400."

2. **OI-02 hypothesis ranking (unverified pending code review).** Every `get_or_create_table` call (six times per query) runs `open_table()`. If LanceDB's Rust crate keeps connection-level caches, in-process state grows with queries. **Candidate causes (ranked):**
   - Table handle re-creation per query (check: does `Table::clone()` create a new reference or duplicate internal state?)
   - LanceDB fragment count growth in a live connection (discriminator: run `inspect_lancedb --graph-population` before/after a 200-record drive; compare fragment and version counts)
   - Tokio task/span accumulation (discriminator: run with `tokio-console`)
   - OpenTelemetry span buffering without release
   - Server-side session state in OpenRouter (discriminator: restart engine mid-drive; if latency resets but query results match, it's in-process)
   **Lines to read:** `engine/src/db/mod.rs:120–178`, `engine/src/workflow/ports.rs` (concrete `DenseRetrievalPort`/`Bm25RetrievalPort` impls), `engine/src/db/DatabaseManager::initialize`. **Verify in docs:** lancedb 0.31 `ConnectBuilder` settings (connection reuse, `read_consistency_interval`, session lifecycle).

3. **D-71: the final-answer line must not introduce a new citation marker.** Verified in code:
   `validate_marker_grounding` (`engine/src/generation/mod.rs` ~:316–374) compares the **set** of `[n]`
   markers found anywhere in `answer` (`extract_inline_markers` ~:424) with the set of `cited_evidence_ids`.
   The check covers the whole answer, not each line, and repeated markers don't count twice. So
   `"… per [1] and [2].\nAnswer: Yes"` with both IDs cited **passes**. The line only adds a rejection if it
   cites a marker the explanation didn't. **Separate risk:** the `Insufficient information.` clause can raise the
   D-69 *empty-citation* classes if the model abstains without citing. See the §4 abstention note (29/43 baseline
   rejections were null_query). **Instruction: the final-answer line carries NO `[n]` markers.**
   That also keeps extraction clean. The non-empty-citation rule (~:209–219) is per answer, so an uncited
   line cannot trigger the D-69 "requires at least one cited evidence ID" rejection by itself.
   **Still to confirm before planning locks the prompt:** the structured-output schema carries the newline
   inside `answer`, and `answer_basis` selection is unchanged by the added instruction. Measure D-69's
   rejection rate before and after on paid drive 1; it must not rise.

4. **Per-mention embeddings bloating the graph budget (D-75 cost creep).** Extracting mentions from the question and embedding each mention separately adds voyageai API calls. For N mentions × per-mention embedding = N extra calls inside graph budget. **Mitigation:** batch all mentions into one embedding call if possible; embed only mentions that fail exact/alias match; do NOT add an unbounded in-process mention→entity cache, because an unbounded per-query cache is exactly the kind of accumulator OI-02 is hunting. **Note:** D-75 permits embedding, just no LLM call; cost is separate from the no-LLM rule. **Line:** engine/src/service.rs:242–273 (being replaced for D-75 seeding).

5. **Degree cap applied after expansion can fail seeding (D-77 hypothesis).** `fetch_neighborhood` errors if frontier size or edge count exceeds `MAX_FRONTIER_SIZE` / `MAX_TOTAL_EDGES` during BFS (doc: engine/src/graph/mod.rs:341–343). On a hub seed (degree 724), a 1-hop fetch alone can exceed bounds. If `attempt_graph_augmentation` maps that error to `AttemptedAndFailed`, high-degree seeds fail silently, contributing to the 9.8% graph presence floor. **Check:** compare max degree (724, p95 10) to `MAX_FRONTIER_SIZE` and `MAX_TOTAL_EDGES` constants; if a hub seed fails pre-fetch, this is expected. **Line:** engine/src/graph/mod.rs:345–533 `fetch_neighborhood`; check constants and compare to D-77's degree distribution baseline.

def extract_final_answer_line(answer_text: str) -> str:
    """
    Extract 'Answer: <short>' from model output.
    - Looks for case-insensitive 'answer:' prefix in the last N lines
    - Returns normalized (squad_normalize'd) text after the label
    - Returns empty string if no line is found

    lines = answer_text.split('\n')

pub async fn attempt_graph_augmentation_mention_based(
    database: &DatabaseManager,
    question: &str,  // raw question text, NOT embedding
    settings: &GraphSettings,
) -> GraphAugmentationOutcome {
    // Step 1: Extract mentions (e.g. capitalized n-grams, 2-10 tokens)
    let mentions = extract_question_mentions(question);  // D-75 Claude's Discretion
    if mentions.is_empty() {
        return GraphAugmentationOutcome::NoMatchFound;
    }
    
    // Step 2: For each mention, try exact/alias match, then name_vector top-k
    let mut all_seeds = Vec::new();
    for mention in mentions {
        // Exact match: build in-memory normalized map or SQL LIKE
        if let Some(entity_id) = exact_or_alias_match(database, &mention).await {
            all_seeds.push(entity_id);
        } else {
            // Embed the mention alone (batching mentioned below)
            if let Ok(mention_embedding) = embed_mention(&mention).await {
                // name_vector top-k on entities table
                if let Some(entity_id) = nearest_entity_by_name_vector(database, &mention_embedding).await {
                    all_seeds.push(entity_id);
                }
            }
        }
    }
    
    if all_seeds.is_empty() {
        return GraphAugmentationOutcome::NoMatchFound;
    }
    
    // Step 3: Seed-to-seed paths ≤2 hops (D-76)
    let path_facts = Vec::new();
    for (i, seed_i) in all_seeds.iter().enumerate() {
        for seed_j in all_seeds.iter().skip(i + 1) {
            // BFS from seed_i, cap degree per node, find path to seed_j
            if let Ok((entities_batch, edges_batch)) = graph::fetch_neighborhood(
                database, seed_i, 1, true
            ).await {
                // narrow_via_cypher for Cypher-confirmed paths
                let (filtered_entities, filtered_edges) =
                    graph::narrow_via_cypher(&entities_batch, &edges_batch, seed_j, 2).await;
                // Extract path facts and inject into RRF (D-76)
                // path_facts.extend(extract_path_facts(...));
            }
        }
    }
    
    // Return graph augmentation outcome
    if path_facts.is_empty() {
        GraphAugmentationOutcome::NoMatchFound
    } else {
        GraphAugmentationOutcome::Success { facts: path_facts }
    }

// Mention extraction heuristic (D-75 Claude's Discretion):
fn extract_question_mentions(question: &str) -> Vec<String> {
    // Example: capitalized n-grams (2–8 tokens), filter out common words
    let words: Vec<&str> = question.split_whitespace().collect();
    let mut mentions = Vec::new();
    for i in 0..words.len() {
        for len in 2..=min(8, words.len() - i) {
            let ngram = &words[i..i + len];
            let joined = ngram.join(" ");
            // Keep if first word is capitalized
            if joined.chars().next().map_or(false, |c| c.is_uppercase()) {
                mentions.push(joined);
            }
        }
    }
    mentions.sort_by_key(|m| std::cmp::Reverse(m.len()));  // longer first
    mentions.dedup();
    mentions
}

**Column (e) rule (closes a yes/no loophole):** on any drive run with the D-71 prompt,
`answer_usable = final_answer_em OR gold_contained(gold, extracted_final_line)`. It is judged on the **extracted
line only**, never on the full explanation, because once the line exists a yes/no gold is easily "contained"
somewhere in a 100-word explanation even when the line says the opposite. A missing line means
`answer_usable = False`. Full-answer `gold_containment` is still computed and reported as a secondary number,
and it is the only (e) proxy available in the D-74 retro, where no line exists. The retro number is labelled as
such and is never compared as-is with drive (e) values.

Rules: rows are regenerated from the journal and the store probes, never hand-edited. The table is emitted
as JSONL plus a markdown yes/no table, not prose. D-34 still applies: an `outcome="error"` arm is excluded
from quality denominators and counted by `error_class`.

**RAG chunking (inherited):**

#### Note: SC-3 floor (D-73). *Proposed; commit in `thresholds.py` before paid drive 1.*

`VECTOR_BASELINE_USABLE_FLOOR = max(0.40, B + 0.10)`, evaluated once and committed as a literal. **B** is the share of G whose gold equals the
modal yes/no label, i.e. what a constant `Answer: Yes` would score. Gold labels alone fix it, so it is known before drive 1.
**On the full non-null sample B = 167/447 = 0.374, which gives a floor ≈ 0.47.** Grounding, all read-only over the 06.3.4 journal
(graph-off, non-null successes, recomputed at spec time):

- **Headline retro:** whole-token full-answer containment **91/147 = 0.619** (Wilson 0.538–0.694). This is scored on the full prose, so it errs high.
- **Stricter proxies:** binary-unambiguous 88/147 = 0.599. Dropping the 9 hits whose gold (`no`) already appears in the question
  text gives **79/147 = 0.537** (Wilson 0.457–0.616). By type: inference 50/51 (entity golds, no echo), comparison 32/68,
  temporal 9/28. Almost all of the signal is in entity golds.
- **Why 0.40:** it is about ¾ of the most conservative proxy. That allows ~25% loss from line non-compliance and line-vs-prose
  disagreement while staying far above EM 0 / F1 0.02.
- **Why B + 0.10:** 62% of non-null golds are yes/no (277/447). A forced short line lets a constant `Yes` score 0.374 with
  no retrieval at all, so 0.40 on its own is barely above guessing. The +0.10 margin is ≈ 1.8 SE at n ≈ 80.
- **Halt risk at the floor that is actually committed, stated for the D-73 commit.** Suppose the true (e) rate equals the
  conservative proxy of 0.537. With n ≈ 80, P(miss) is about **13% at 0.474** and **25% at 0.50** (the floor if B ≈ 0.40).
  Against a bare 0.40 it would be under 1%. The committed floor sits above the proxy's Wilson lower bound (0.457).
  That is the price of the chance guard, and it is deliberately **not** lowered to cut halt risk.
- **Why a halt may be the right outcome:** the retro's binary-type containment sat *below* the constant-Yes rate for its type.
  Comparison scored 0.47 against 112/179 = 0.63; temporal scored 0.32 against 55/110 = 0.50. If the forced line does not lift
  binary strata above chance, SC-3 halts at D-84, which is the intended outcome.

#### Note: making D-64 flatness executable

- `RunRecord` has no `ordinal`, so drive 1 runs as **one** invocation with `--workers 1 --retries 0 --no-resume` (the CLI
  defaults are `--retries 2` and `--resume`, so both flags must be explicit). A thin adapter assigns ordinals in journal order,
  with `segment="segment-1"` and `restart_ordinal=None`.
- **False-flat trap:** any RetrieveHybrid failure with `error_kind == 1` censors the set. `analyze_decay` then returns both
  prongs `is_available=False` **and `verdict_decay_present=False`**. Never read that boolean alone: an unavailable verdict means
  *not proven*, and that is an SC-2 miss. Do not relax `decay.py`.
- **Put this to the user at the drive-1 checkpoint:** in practice flatness needs **zero RetrieveHybrid timeouts across ~200
  records** under the re-derived p95×1.5 budgets.
- **SC-2 miss handling** has no locked rule. *Proposed:* the D-83 shape, i.e. one root-cause iteration on OI-02 and then a fresh
  D-86 authorisation. It is never a restart, a budget raise or a retry.
