## Halt: what stopped the plan and why

Preflight ran once (2026-09-29T16:39:56Z to 16:40:19Z) and failed two checks:

- **`corpus_generation`: "No index_generation observed in preflight query snapshot."** Measured cause: the engine's first query (correlation `f2493e90-06fe-4588-b81b-ce050ee1dda8`) ended after `RetrieveHybrid`. The Jaeger `prompt_assembly` span ran **78.6 ms** with `node AssemblePrompt failed: timeout`, and `workflow_checkpoints` for that trace stop at `post_retrievehybrid`. `prompt_timeout_ms` is the user's 65 ms floor, set at 1.5 x pass A's 43 ms measured maximum. Pass A's warm-up records, which the harness excludes, were 55 ms and 74 ms (records 1 and 2), the probe's record 1 was 41 ms and the confirmation's was 46 ms, and steady state is about 1 ms. So the first `AssemblePrompt` on a fresh engine carries a one-off cost that the derivation never saw. Preflight has no warm-up, and neither will a first real query. The lazy `cl100k_base_singleton()` tokenizer build is the candidate source; it was not isolated.
- **`canary_floors`:** canary `mhr-0d5e238015ef` (graph-on) observed 0 graph nodes. That query completed in 2.26 s with a `GRAPH_UNAVAILABLE` notice, its traversal took about 0.13 s and nothing timed out, so it is the graph-yield question and not a budget effect. **It predates this plan:** the same question's graph-on record (ordinal 52) carries `GRAPH_UNAVAILABLE` and 0 graph nodes in pass A, in the 06.3.4.1-25 probe and in the 06.3.4.1-26 confirmation, all on the same `lance-702` store. No earlier SUMMARY records a `canary_floors` result on `multihop_rag_diag`, so whether that preflight has ever passed is unknown. Fixing the first-query `AssemblePrompt` cost alone would therefore not make preflight pass; the graph floor needs its own reading (or its own decision) as well.

Per the plan's orchestrator instruction and D-86, nothing was answered with a raise or an override, and the preflight was not re-run. The engine (PID 17908) and gateway (PID 32340) were stopped, `tasklist` shows no `engine.exe` or `gateway.exe`, the identity check passes again, and Docker is left up.

**Spend:** OpenRouter `auth/key` usage 2.281979791 before, 2.285930711 after (the account lags: the reading taken immediately after was still 2.281979791). **Delta $0.00395 against $0.05 authorised, within cap.**
