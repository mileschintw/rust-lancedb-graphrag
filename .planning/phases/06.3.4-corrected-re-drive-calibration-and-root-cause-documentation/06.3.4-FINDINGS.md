## 3. Paired-Comparison Disposition

The paired comparison evaluates the 143 answerable questions where both `graph-on` and `graph-off` arms completed successfully:

| **Recall@4** | **0.3648** | **0.3613** | **+0.0035** | Marginal Positive (+0.35% retrieval recall) |
| **Precision@4** | **0.2168** | **0.2150** | **+0.0017** | Marginal Positive (+0.17% context precision) |

### Negative / Zero Delta Root Cause Investigation
1. **The Single-Entity Cosine Bottleneck**:  
   In `engine/src/service.rs:242` (`attempt_graph_augmentation`), the engine passes the embedding of the entire multi-hop question to `nearest_to(query_embedding).column("name_vector").limit(1)`. Multi-hop questions involve relationships between distinct entities (e.g. "Who directed the movie starring X?"). The embedding of a full question rarely achieves a cosine similarity $\ge 0.5$ (`seed_match_min_score`) with a short entity name (e.g. "Christopher Nolan"), resulting in `NoMatchFound` in over 90% of queries.
2. **Noise Injection on Non-Matching Seeds**:  
   In the 14 cases where an entity did match, fetching its 1-hop neighborhood without relation-filtering often injected general entity trivia into the prompt rather than the specific bridge relation required by the question, slightly reducing generation token F1 (-0.0024).
