<objective>
Drive the staged prefix, compute the two numbers the staged gate exists to check, compare them
against the floors committed before any of them existed, record the verdict together with what the
staged stage actually cost — and close the plan on the decision to spend, so that decision sits on
a plan boundary rather than in the middle of one.

Purpose: the 2026-09-03 collapse was not visible until the run was over. D-44 exists so that a
partial fix is caught on a hundred queries instead of a thousand. The staged stage is also the only
place a defensible estimate of the full drive's cost can come from: the superseded run's `$0.0199`
is not a comparison point, because that run was cheap precisely because 966 of its queries generated
nothing at all.

Everything this plan asserts in advance is a **threshold**. Everything it produces is a **measured
value** and is recorded, never predicted.

Output: the staged records in the corrected run directory, the machine-readable verdict beside them,
and `06.3.4-STAGED-GATE.md` carrying the observations, the comparisons, the spend and the dated
decision.
</objective>
