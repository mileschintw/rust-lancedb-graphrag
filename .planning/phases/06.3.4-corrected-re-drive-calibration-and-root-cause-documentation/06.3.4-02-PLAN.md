<objective>
Give the report the two agreement statistics the calibration decision actually requires, on the
standard library, before there is any human score to compute them from.

Purpose: D-48 requires quadratic weighted kappa **and** Spearman rank correlation on the paired
human and judge integers, against a target of at-or-above `0.70`, with judged numbers published as
uncalibrated below it. The harness computes neither today — `score.py`'s calibration block
(`score.py:448-519`) carries only an exact-match rate and a mean absolute deviation, and
`dimensions.py`'s judged builders (`dimensions.py:150-241`) expose only those two as detail floats.
Neither answers the question the target is stated against. The stated evaluation contract also
forbids adding a dependency, so both statistics are written here rather than imported.

This plan runs before any human score exists, deliberately. Writing the statistic after seeing the
ratings is how a target quietly becomes whatever the ratings happened to produce.

**Where the uncalibrated label lives is a schema constraint, not a preference.** Both cross-AI
reviewers independently reproduced the same fatal error against the live tree: `DimensionResult`
(`eval/src/lancet_eval/dimensions.py:14-41`) has `model_config = ConfigDict(extra="forbid")` and a
`_validate_consistency` model-validator whose first branch raises `status 'ok' cannot have a reason`.
`make_groundedness_result` / `make_faithfulness_result` return `status="ok"` with a mean score
whenever verdicts exist, so a scored judged dimension is *always* `ok`. Putting the uncalibrated
sentence in `reason` therefore raises a `pydantic_core.ValidationError` during scoring; and even if
it did not, `templates/report.md.j2:38-42` renders `format_details(d.detail)` rather than `reason`
for an `ok` row, so it would be invisible. `extra="forbid"` also rules out inventing a field on
`DimensionResult` without moving the schema and the template with it.

The resolution this plan takes is the one with the smallest blast radius: machine-readable values go
into `DimensionResult.detail`, which is already `dict[str, float]` and already carries
`calibration_exact_match` / `calibration_mad`; the human-readable sentence goes into
`RunMetadata.notes`, which already exists (`report.py:45`) and is already rendered conditionally
(`report.md.j2:30`). The one genuinely new schema field is the completed-dual-score count on
`RunMetadata`, which drags `report.md.j2` and `eval/report.schema.json` with it — all three are in
`files_modified`. `DimensionResult` and its validator are not touched at all.

Output: `agreement.py`, the wiring through scoring and the report surface, and golden-vector tests.
</objective>
