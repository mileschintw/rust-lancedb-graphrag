<objective>
Close the two threats that block Phase 06.3.4: a judged-spend meter that stops metering the moment a
provider omits `usage`, and a judged-candidate selection that sizes its slice against a larger
population than it will actually judge.

Purpose: both were found after gap-closure plans 08/09 landed, by the code-review and security-audit
gates running in parallel. T-06.3.4-25 is a control that looks present but cannot fire — once a
provider response arrives without a `usage` block, `accumulated_judge_spend` freezes and every
remaining question in the judged slice dispatches as a real, unmetered, paid call. T-06.3.4-42 is a
regression, not a new defect: it was closed 2026-09-10 on the caveat "not yet exercised in
production — judging deferred," and plan 06.3.4-09 wired `derive_judged_slice_size` into a live
production caller for the first time, activating the exact path the mitigation was supposed to
guard. Neither gap is a test failure; both are paths no test ever drove, which is why the suite is
green and the threats are open.

Output: a spend meter that advances on every dispatched call and discloses when it estimated rather
than observed, and one judgeable predicate used by candidate selection, by the judge loop, and by
the worksheet selection instead of three restatements that can and did diverge.
</objective>
