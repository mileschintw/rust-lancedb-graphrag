<objective>
Drive the whole committed sample across both arms into the run directory the staged stage already
opened, prove the merged journal is complete and undamaged, and publish the corrected report.

Purpose: this is the run Phase 6.4 documents for v1 milestone closure. Everything before it was
either machinery or a sample small enough to be thrown away. Three things therefore have to hold at
once: the spend must stay inside the cap a human set at 06.3.4-03 against numbers they could see;
the merged journal must be a clean superset of the staged prefix, because a duplicated or dropped
work unit corrupts every denominator downstream; and the report must actually render, which it
cannot do until the journal that began as a staged prefix is reconciled to publishable.

Publication happens here and deliberately not in 06.3.4-03. A staged journal cannot be rendered at
all, and staged numbers are gate inputs rather than results — publishing them would put a
provisional figure into the record under the same name the real one will carry.

Judging is not in this plan. It is its own capped stage owned by 06.3.4-05, so a judged-spend
overrun cannot ride in on the back of the deterministic drive.

Output: the completed journal, the reconciled header, and the published `report.json`, `report.md`
and `metadata.json` in the corrected run directory.
</objective>
