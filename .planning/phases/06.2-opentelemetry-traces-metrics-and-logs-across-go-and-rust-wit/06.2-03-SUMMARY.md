Derived transformation rules from the live scrape:
- Source instrument periods (`.`) are sanitized to underscores (`_`) with no extra Collector namespace prefix.
- Monotonic counters append `_total` (e.g. `lancet_rag_retrieval_path_failures_total`, `lancet_ingest_documents_total`).
- Histograms append `_bucket`, `_count`, `_sum` (e.g. `lancet_rag_evidence_set_size_bucket`).
- Duration histograms with `ms` unit are expanded by Prometheus exposition to `_milliseconds` (e.g. `lancet_rag_query_duration_milliseconds`, `lancet_index_rebuild_duration_milliseconds`).
- Gauges export directly under their sanitized name (e.g. `lancet_index_corpus_generation`).
*(Note: Earlier draft wording assumed an extra `namespace: lancet` prefix in collector config and `_ms` unit suffixes, which caused double-prefixing and panel mismatches until corrected by Plan 07).*
