## Observed Prometheus Metric Transformation Rule
OTel instrument names exported via the Collector Prometheus exporter (`namespace: lancet`, endpoint `0.0.0.0:8889`) follow the canonical transformation:
- Periods (`.`) are replaced with underscores (`_`).
- Counters append `_total` (e.g. `lancet_rag_retrieval_path_failures_total`).
- Histograms append `_bucket`, `_count`, `_sum` (e.g. `lancet_rag_query_duration_ms_count`, `lancet_rag_evidence_set_size_bucket`).
- Millisecond duration units append `_ms` when declared in the instrument.
