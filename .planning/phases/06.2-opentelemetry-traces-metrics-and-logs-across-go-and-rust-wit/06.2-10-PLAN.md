## Phase User Story

**As an** operator looking at a slow `/rag/query` trace in Grafana, **I want to** click "Logs for this span" and land on that trace's Loki lines, **so that** trace, log and metric panes correlate on one `trace_id` without hand-copying IDs (G-06.2-1, D-34, D-40).

<objective>
Close G-06.2-1: remove the unresolvable `tags: [{key: trace_id, value: trace_id}]` mapping from the Jaeger datasource's `tracesToLogsV2` block so Grafana stops suppressing the trace-to-logs action, and lock the corrected shape with a committed assertion.

Purpose: trace ID is intrinsic OTel span context (`SpanContext.trace_id`), not a span attribute. Grafana evaluates `tags` against span attributes and hides the "Logs for this span" action when a configured tag variable cannot be interpolated. `filterByTraceID: true` is the built-in mechanism that queries Loki by trace ID with no `tags` entry at all — so the mapping was not merely redundant, it was the suppressor.
Output: a three-line deletion in one provisioning file, one Rust regression test, a re-measured engine pin, and one VALIDATION.md map row updated in place.
</objective>
