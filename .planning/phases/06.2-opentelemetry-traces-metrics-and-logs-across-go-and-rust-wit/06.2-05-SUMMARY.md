# Phase 06.2 Plan 05 Summary: Operational Metrics & Dashboard Contract

## Executive Overview
Plan 05 implements the authoritative 10-instrument operational metric surface (D-35) and verifies its complete alignment with Grafana operations dashboard queries:
1. **10 Authoritative Metric Instruments & Bounded Dimensions**: Populated `engine/src/telemetry/metrics.rs` with static instruments matching the exact names and units from Plan 06.2-03:
   - `lancet.rag.query.duration` (histogram, ms, `outcome`: completed/failed)
   - `lancet.rag.retrieval.path_failures` (counter, `path`: dense/bm25/graph, `kind`: timeout/error/unavailable)
   - `lancet.rag.answer.degraded` (counter, `answer_basis`: retrieval/mixed/model_only)
   - `lancet.rag.citation.repairs` (counter, `action`: repaired/dropped)
   - `lancet.rag.generation.retries` (counter, `outcome`: recovered/exhausted)
   - `lancet.rag.evidence.set_size` (histogram, count)
   - `lancet.ingest.documents` (counter, `outcome`: completed/failed)
   - `lancet.ingest.chunks` (counter, count)
   - `lancet.index.rebuild.duration` (histogram, ms, `outcome`: completed/failed)
   - `lancet.index.corpus_generation` (gauge, numeric `nodes_version`)
2. **Query-Path Decision Site Instrumentation**:
   - `retrieve.rs`: Records path failure increments at `RetrievalDegradedDense` and `RetrievalDegradedBm25` notice sites with matching kind (`timeout`, `unavailable`, `error`); no recording at `NoEvidence`.
   - `graph_context.rs`: Records path failure increments at `GraphUnavailable`, `GraphTimeout`, and `GraphDegraded` notice sites; records nothing at operator-requested `GraphAblation`.
   - `generate.rs`: Records generation retries (`recovered` vs `exhausted`) on retry execution; records citation repair actions (`repaired` vs `dropped`).
   - `assemble_prompt.rs`: Records `lancet.rag.evidence.set_size` with post-packing evidence block count (or 0 for empty evidence).
   - `runner.rs`: Records `lancet.rag.answer.degraded` at terminal emission when `answer_basis` is `mixed` or `model_only`.
3. **Ingestion & Index Rebuild Instrumentation**:
   - `ingest.rs`: Records `lancet.ingest.documents` (`completed` vs `failed`) and `lancet.ingest.chunks` at worker completion/failure; captures rebuild duration on all exit paths of `rebuild_and_swap` and records numeric `nodes_version` on `lancet.index.corpus_generation` after swap.
   - `main.rs`: Records initial `lancet.index.corpus_generation` gauge upon building initial BM25 snapshot.
4. **Dashboard Cross-Check & Comprehensive Verification**: Added 18 unit tests in `engine/src/tests/telemetry_metrics.rs` validating registry completeness, bounded attributes, zero-identifier privacy protection, two-valued query outcomes, exact recording behaviors across all nodes, and validating that all 10 panels in `deploy/grafana/dashboards/lancet-rag-operations.json` resolve to the defined instruments. All 422 engine lib tests pass. Updated `scripts/engine-test-targets.sh` gate to 462 total tests.
