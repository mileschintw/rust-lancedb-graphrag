## Accomplishments

- **Arm registry (D-149, D-140, D-141).** Five lever arms; `hybrid+graph-v2` is present because the committed selection is `graph_list_precision`. `ArmSpec.levers` refuses duplicates, non-canonical order, unknown names and `graph_v2` with the graph off (D-165). `request_fields` adds `levers` for a lever arm only, so every legacy label and `hybrid` send the exact 06.3.5 body. `LEGACY_ARM_LABELS` keeps the judged-stage sizes at four arms.
- **Provenance (D-134, D-161).** Clause h (`lever_echo_failures`, a function, never message text), clause i (count-and-exclude per arm), clause j (a rerank leak either way), and clause e now also flags GRAPH_ABLATION on a graph-on arm. The 06.3.5 journals re-score identically.
- **D-73 at drive time.** A `heldout` or `rehearsal` corpus listing a lever arm under the 06.3.5 token is refused; any other token must resolve and its arm set must equal the pre-registration's. Both refusals run after the ordering gate and before the first request; `dev` is exempt.
- **Preflight (O10 / D-174).** Degraded rerank canaries are reported per arm and pooled rather than failed one by one; the check fails on an arm with no successful rerank canary, or when the pooled count reaches the O10 count (resolved pre-registration, else the constant 5). Clauses h and j still fail on any canary.
- **Spend (O15 / D-176).** A golden of every committed journal's `compute_spend`, recorded at the base commit before the change, pins byte-identical pricing.
- **Tripwire (O10 / D-174).** `RerankTripwire` compares exact fractions with a strict `>`: 11 of 50 halts, 10 of 50 does not, 15 of 49 does not, the fifth consecutive degrade halts; `rerank_tripwire_for` returns None for `dev`, the 06.3.5 object and an unresolvable token.
- **SC-2 floor (D-151 / D-164) and O14.** The error-mode clause fails a dominant timeout only at `timeouts / n >= 1/40` under a lever pre-registration, per arm and pooled on its own n, and reads `reported: timeout dominant, not material (k of n)` below it. The 06.3.5 held-out journal re-reads with exactly its recorded verdicts, and a hypothetical re-read under the floor reproduces the AI-SPEC table (dense-only 4, hybrid 6, hybrid+graph 2 and pooled 12 of 1404 read reported). Rerank-bearing arms print the subtracted flatness series in `detail` and at the end of `reason`; it never changes the status.
- **Classes.** `FamilySpec` and `LeverPreRegistration` are frozen dataclasses with the AI-SPEC field list; no instance and no 06.3.6 token string in `thresholds.py` (`git log -S` empty).

## Decisions Made

- `hybrid+graph-v2` registration keys off `client.LEVER_ORDER` (plan 05 declared `graph_v2` from the same selection); the test reads the selection JSON, so a selection of `none` with a stale LEVER_ORDER fails loudly instead of the registry parsing `.planning/` at import.
- Tripwire counts are per invocation. A resumed drive starts from zero because resuming after a halt is the owner's re-drive decision (D-110); journalled degraded records are unaffected.
- An attempt is a rerank-arm record carrying rerank telemetry or code 23. The plan wrote "records with `workflow_meta.rerank`"; counting a notice-only degraded record too keeps the rate at or below 1 and matches clause j (which accepts either).
- `compute_spend` includes rerank spend with `include_embeddings=False` (the flag drops only the embedding estimate).
- The floor reading keeps legacy `reason`/`detail` byte-identical (floor keys are added only for a floored reading), so recorded gate files and re-reads do not move.
- Rerank outcomes arrive lower-case on the wire (`completed`, `degraded_timeout`); the preflight compares case-insensitively.
