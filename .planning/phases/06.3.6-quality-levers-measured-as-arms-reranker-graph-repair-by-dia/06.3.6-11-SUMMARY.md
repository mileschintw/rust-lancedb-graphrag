**`PromptOptions` threaded to both prompt builders, with `SOURCE`/`DOC_TITLE`/`PUBLISHED` evidence headers and two lever policy sentences appended after the untouched base policy, plus an immutable per-snapshot `DocMetaMap` attached only under the `evidence_metadata` lever so retrieval is untouched by construction.**

## Accomplishments

- Levers 3 and 4 change only the prompt. `PromptOptions::from_levers(ctx.levers)` is computed in `AssemblePromptNode`, at both `GenerationRequest` construction sites (`GenerateAnswerNode` and `workflow/mod.rs`) and read by `pack_openrouter_messages`, so the workflow prompt and the provider messages cannot disagree.
- Metadata headers render after `<TITLE>` in the order SOURCE, DOC_TITLE, PUBLISHED. All seven non-empty subsets and the empty case are pinned; the empty case equals the flag-off bytes written out literally in the test.
- The policy is base, graph sentence, metadata sentence, format rules, each after `"\n"`. `base_system_policy()` has no changed line (`git diff 9583bf80` over `prompt.rs` shows none), the first `then end with: Answer: ` is still the grounded policy's, and `generation/openrouter.rs` holds no `CancellationToken::new()`.
- `CorpusSnapshot.doc_meta: Arc<DocMetaMap>` is empty from `new`, set by `with_doc_meta`, and carried by all five ingest rebuild literals as `Arc::clone(&prior.doc_meta)`. A rebuild test over the success path and over three degraded paths (injected fault, checkout failure, graph build failure) shows the same `Arc`.
- Retrieval is unchanged by construction. With a metadata-bearing snapshot, `evidence_metadata` alone and `binary_answer_format` alone leave the dense list, BM25 list, final list and the whole snapshot (result hash and cited chunks included) equal to the `graph_on_node_chain` section of `retrieve_node_default.golden`, and the ranking and result hash equal the lever-free run.
- Availability: `evidence_metadata` is `lever_unavailable` over an empty map and admitted over a non-empty one; `binary_answer_format` now reaches `GenerationRequest.prompt_options`.

- The wording of `EVIDENCE_METADATA_POLICY_SENTENCE` is the AI-SPEC section 4 item 4d candidate verbatim. `BINARY_ANSWER_FORMAT_RULES` is written to the five constraints of item 5 and each is asserted on the sentence. Both are candidates, revised on dev and frozen in the D-154 commit.
