| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Harness estimate (`compute_spend`, the figure the cap is checked against) | **$0.44445** of $2.00 (22.2%); not a lower bound |

The two numbers measure different things. `compute_spend` prices every token at the pinned constants (output 1.28 per 1M, never below
the committed listing, D-173) plus ceilings for failed or unreported calls, so it is a conservative upper bound that bounds the cap.
The account figure is what the key was actually billed (all stages that bill this key: generation, rerank and embeddings), and it is
about 3.9 times lower, which is consistent with the Sail Research rate sitting below the pinned constant. The baseline reading was
unsettled and taken 30 s after preflight 3, so the settled delta includes at most a few tenths of a cent of preflight 3.
