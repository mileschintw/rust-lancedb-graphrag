## 1. Executive Summary

On 2026-09-03, the initial evaluation harness run for `multihop_rag` published a report claiming 1,000 successful queries with high benchmark scores. In reality, **966 of the 1,000 queries produced empty answers (`answer: ""`) and retrieved zero valid evidence**. 

The root cause of this total failure was twofold:
1. **Mis-sized Engine Timeout Budgets**: Production workflow node timeout budgets were derived from development unit tests rather than multi-hop retrieval workloads over LanceDB. When queries took longer than the tight timeout thresholds, the engine aborted execution before retrieval or generation could finish. However, due to an unhandled stream truncation bug in the gateway-to-harness bridge, these early aborts were recorded as successful queries with empty string answers.
2. **Unreliable Ablation Delta over an Empty Join**: The published graph ablation delta claimed a positive effect for knowledge graph augmentation. In truth, the join between `graph-on` and `graph-off` arms contained virtually zero completed pairs. The ablation score was computed over non-comparable empty records, producing an illusion of graph effectiveness while 99% of graph operations had actually timed out (`GRAPH_TIMEOUT`).

## 3. The Decay Signature

During initial post-mortem reviews, an apparent "decay signature" was observed where queries early in the drive succeeded more often than queries later in the drive.

To settle whether this was caused by cumulative memory/connection leakage or process state, the dedicated measurement pass in Phase 06.3.3 rigorously tested this phenomenon. As documented in `.planning/phases/06.3.3-timeout-budget-derivation-and-operational-envelope-re-anch/06.3.3-MEASUREMENT.md`:
* **Landed Verdict**: `decay_present: False`.
* **The Supported Hypothesis**: `06.3.3-MEASUREMENT.md` established that the latency tail was primarily driven by query complexity and `process_state` rather than simple operating system resource leakage. 
* However, as discovered during the Phase 06.3.4 re-drive, successive LanceDB queries across hundreds of records in an un-compacted index still exhibit steady latency creep (growing from 7.4s to >30s over 400 queries), proving that long-running drives require both sufficient timeout headroom and active index resource management.

---

## 4. False-Green Breakdown

Three core metrics created the false-green appearance of the 2026-09-03 report:

1. **Wire Contract Conformance**:  
   The harness checked only HTTP 200 response codes and JSON parsing. Because the engine streamed partial events and closed with an internal gRPC error, the gateway emitted a 200 status with an empty body rather than a 500 error, marking failed runs as `outcome: "success"`.
2. **Answer Exact Match & F1**:  
   Empty gold answers were scored against empty generated answers, yielding false Exact Match credits, or denominators were computed only over the tiny 34-record survivor set without penalizing the 966 missing answers.
3. **Graph Ablation Delta**:  
   Because both `graph-on` and `graph-off` failed identically on 96.6% of questions, comparing the two arms showed near-zero variance, which the un-paired metric calculation interpreted as consistent parity rather than complete collapse.

---

## 5. Ablation Disjointness Analysis

In the 2026-09-03 run, the graph ablation delta was computed by taking global averages across all `graph-on` records and all `graph-off` records independently, rather than joining strictly paired records per question:
* Out of 500 questions, only **11 questions** had completed answers in both arms.
* The remaining 489 questions were either completely empty or had only one arm survive.
* By averaging unpaired populations with high dropout rates, the resulting delta was statistically meaningless and untethered to actual graph augmentation benefits.

## 6. Knowledge Graph Health Breakdown

A critical question was whether the graph store itself was populated or whether the failure stemmed from missing entities.

As verified by `.planning/phases/06.3.3-timeout-budget-derivation-and-operational-envelope-re-anch/06.3.3-STORE-BASELINE.md`:
* **Landed Store Population**: `graph_disposition: populated`, `unpopulated: false`.
* LanceDB `entities.lance` contains **27,971 entities** and `entity_edges.lance` contains **29,566 edges**.
* The breakdown in the 2026-09-03 run showed that the failure was operational:
  * `GRAPH_TIMEOUT`: 494 queries. The initial timeout budget of 4,000 ms was an order of magnitude too short for LanceDB vector search and multi-hop Cypher traversal.
  * `GRAPH_UNAVAILABLE`: 4 queries.
  * `NoMatchFound`: **0 recorded**, because every query timed out before the distance check could complete.

---

## 7. Impact on the D-32 Floor

Under Decision D-32, an empirical floor of `graph_presence_rate >= 0.20` (20%) was established to ensure knowledge graph retrieval actively participates in answering queries.

In the 2026-09-03 run, because 494 of 500 graph queries timed out at 4.0s, the true `NoMatchFound` rate was completely unknowable. Traversal was aborted by the clock before the engine could determine whether matching entities existed.

---

## 8. Corrections and Findings from the Corrected Re-drive

Phase 06.3.1 introduced essential architectural repairs:
* **D-01 (Wire Capture)**: Guaranteed capture of stream abortions and engine errors as durable `outcome: "error"` records.
* **D-28 (`NOTICE_CODE_RETRIEVAL_FAILED` / `RETRIEVAL_FAILED`)**: Emitted typed error notices whenever `RetrieveHybrid` fails.
* **D-34 / D-37 (Honest Denominators & Strict Pairing)**: Mandated that all ablation metrics join strictly on question IDs where both arms completed, with unpaired drops visibly counted.
* **D-17 / D-33 (Timeout Budgets & Partial Snapshot Provenance)**: Re-derived timeout budgets (e.g. `graph_operation_timeout_ms = 40000`, `retrieve_timeout_ms = 30000`) and preserved `index_generation` on partial snapshots.

### Dispositions Quoted from Phase 06.3.4 Findings
The corrected re-drive in Phase 06.3.4 (`06.3.4-FINDINGS.md`) produced the first honest empirical measurements on `multihop_rag`:

   "Observed Presence Rate: `0.0979` (9.8%) ... Disposition: Missed Floor (`floor_miss: 1.0`). In 129 of 143 evaluated pairs (90.2%), graph traversal returned either `GRAPH_TIMEOUT` (89 records) or `GRAPH_UNAVAILABLE` / zero nodes (40 records)."

   "Paired Delta: Exact Match delta = `+0.0000`, Token SQuAD F1 delta = `-0.0024`. Single-vector nearest neighbor match against entity names fails on multi-hop queries, resulting in `NoMatchFound` in >90% of queries, and noise injection in the remaining 9.8%."
