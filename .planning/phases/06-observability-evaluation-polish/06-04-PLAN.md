## Phase User Story

**As a** client of Lancet's `/rag/query` endpoint, **I want to** receive an honest, machine-readable
answer even when the graph is unavailable, one retrieval path fails, evidence is absent, or the
model's citations do not resolve, **so that** I can tell a degraded answer from a healthy one — and a
grounded answer from a model-only one — without parsing prose.

> Planner-derived from the phase's only user-facing outcome (RAG-03 degraded mode). This plan is a
> pure refactor and delivers no slice of that story directly; it prepares the surface where the
> story's `typed_code` and `metadata` fields will land (D-81/D-82).

<objective>
Split the low-churn half of `gateway/main.go` into `internal/` packages: configuration loading, SSE
writing and the response DTO surface, plus the reserved telemetry directory.

Purpose: D-82 requires the Go gateway to be restructured before telemetry work lands, "rather than
growing past 1,500 lines". Research §2 measured the coupling and recommends splitting on the churn
line: `main_test.go` names `writeWorkflowEventSSE`, `writeStreamErrorSSE`, `noticeDTO`,
`structuredCitationDTO`, `retrievalSnapshotDTO` and `ragQueryRequestBody` zero times and `loadConfig`
twice, so this half moves with roughly five test edits. `internal/engineclient` is the high-churn half
and is plan 06-05.
Output: three new `internal/` packages, `main.go` reduced by its config and SSE surface, and
`scripts/gateway-test-targets.sh` as the reusable per-package invariant gate.
</objective>

<scope_rationale>
Three tasks: the per-package gate first (so the before-state is recorded against an untouched tree),
then `internal/config` (the cleanest seam — `Config` is already exported-shaped with `mapstructure`
tags), then `internal/sse` plus the `internal/telemetry` stub (zero direct test references, so the
diff is mechanical).

`internal/engineclient` is deliberately absent. Research measured 50 `app{` composite literals and 24
`IngestOutcome` references in `main_test.go`; moving the engine interface out of `package main` forces
roughly 74 mechanical test edits, and mixing that with this plan would make both diffs unreviewable.
It is plan 06-05.

The `app` struct, the four HTTP handlers, the document store and the reconciler also stay in
`package main`. D-82 names exactly four packages — telemetry setup, SSE handling, engine client and
config — and this plan plus 06-05 deliver exactly those four. Extracting a fifth is discretionary
scope the decision does not ask for.

`go-guidelines.md` applies here and the target is Go **1.25** (`gateway/go.mod:3` declares
`go 1.25.0`; the installed toolchain is newer). Go 1.26 features are forbidden even though they would
compile — in particular the pointer-to-expression form of `new` and the generic `errors` type
assertion. Both are relevant in plan 06-07, not here, but the target applies to every Go edit.

Shell note: every `<automated>` gate in this phase is POSIX shell, matching `.planning/config.json`'s
`test_command` (`… && (cd gateway && go test ./...)`). Run them through the Bash tool, not PowerShell.
</scope_rationale>
