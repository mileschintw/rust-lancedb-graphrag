## Phase User Story

**As a** client of Lancet's `/rag/query` endpoint, **I want to** receive an honest, machine-readable
answer even when the graph is unavailable, one retrieval path fails, evidence is absent, or the
model's citations do not resolve, **so that** I can tell a degraded answer from a healthy one — and a
grounded answer from a model-only one — without parsing prose.

> Planner-derived. The ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 6 is not written in user-story form; this
> story was derived from the phase's only user-facing outcome (RAG-03 degraded mode) rather than
> invented. Flagged as an assumption in the planning return.

<objective>
Close the first half of DEBT-P3-MODULE-GRAPH by moving the chunker and the entire configuration
surface out of the binary target and into the `engine` library crate, without changing a single
observable behavior.

Purpose: D-81 front-loads the module-graph restructure as a pure-refactor plan so that the wire
contract and the four RAG-03 behavior clauses land on a settled foundation and any later suite
regression is attributable. D-80's acceptance criterion is literally "binary imports shared modules
from library crate"; this plan performs the two smallest, independently revertible steps of that
move and establishes the per-target test-count gate every later restructure step re-runs.
Output: `engine::chunker` and `engine::config` declared by the library, `main.rs` importing both,
and `scripts/engine-test-targets.sh` as the reusable per-target invariant gate.
</objective>
