## Phase User Story

**As a** client of Lancet's `/rag/query` endpoint, **I want to** receive an honest, machine-readable
answer even when the graph is unavailable, one retrieval path fails, evidence is absent, or the
model's citations do not resolve, **so that** I can tell a degraded answer from a healthy one — and a
grounded answer from a model-only one — without parsing prose.

> Third and final gap closure for SC3 and SC5. 06-13 and 06-14 both returned `partial` because their
> proofs were test doubles that never reached the layer that rejects the input. This plan moves the
> failing gate and proves the move through the real provider adapter.

<objective>
Close the two remaining 06-VERIFICATION.md gaps — SC3 (D-10/D-11/D-12) and SC5 (D-14) — by moving
grounding validation's four marker checks out of the provider adapter and into the workflow seam
that owns repair and basis decisions, and by making the engine (not the model) decide `answer_basis`
on an opted-in empty-evidence run.

Purpose: `OpenRouterGenerator::execute_one_call` runs the complete grounding validator on the RAW
model output at `openrouter.rs:792`. Every SC3 and SC5 behavior this phase was supposed to ship sits
downstream of a gate that rejects its own inputs. `citation_repair_enabled` defaults true
(`config.rs:143-145`) and `OpenRouterGenerator` is the sole wired production generator
(`main.rs:96`), so this is the default production configuration.

Output: a shape-only adapter gate, a gated inline remainder path, an engine-decided model-only
contract at both validation sites, the D-19-permitted prompt sentence, and five node-level
mock-server tests that drive the real adapter end to end for both gaps.
</objective>
