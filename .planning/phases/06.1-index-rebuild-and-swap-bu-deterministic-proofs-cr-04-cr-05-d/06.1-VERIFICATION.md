### Gaps Summary

Three of the seven ROADMAP success criteria fail, all rooted in (or adjacent to) one structural issue: `engine/src/service.rs`'s `ProductionDenseRetrievalPort` and `engine/src/ingest.rs`'s `rebuild_and_swap` both operate on `.clone()`s of a single `lancedb::Table` handle constructed once in `main.rs` and shared across every in-flight query and the periodic rebuild task. Because `lancedb::Table::clone()` shares one mutable `pinned_version` cell rather than creating an independently-versioned view, `Table::checkout(v)` calls from concurrent callers race on that shared cell — defeating the "exactly one generation, never mixed" guarantee that SC2 and SC3 require, on the routine path (a query in flight during a debounced rebuild) this whole phase exists to handle. This effectively violates two of Plan 06.1-01's own frontmatter prohibitions (see Prohibitions table above): the checkout-isolation prohibition was followed to the letter (clone-then-checkout) but its underlying assumption about what `.clone()` isolates was wrong, and the never-mix prohibition fails as a direct consequence. It is documented in detail, with exact vendored-crate line citations, in `06.1-REVIEW.md` CR-01/CR-02, and was independently re-verified by this verifier by reading the cited call sites directly (`engine/src/main.rs:30,100,133`; `engine/src/service.rs:82-88,502-538`; `engine/src/ingest.rs:1454-1459`) — the review's claim holds.

A second, narrower and independent gap affects SC4: `rebuild_and_swap` discards the `Result` of `checkout_latest()` (`let _ = nodes_latest.checkout_latest().await;`, `ingest.rs:1455`), so a genuine checkout failure is reported as a successful rebuild instead of triggering the degraded-snapshot / warning-notice path SC4 requires. The notice-emission machinery itself (`NoticeCode::IndexRebuildFailed` at `NoticeSeverity::Warning` from `retrieve.rs:66-70`) is correctly implemented and reachable for the `Bm25Index::from_table` failure path — the gap is specifically the discarded `checkout_latest()` error, not the notice plumbing.
