### Prior Verification Context

The previous round (superseded on disk, commit `9533ef0`) scored 4/7 and FAILED SC2, SC3, SC4 on a
structural finding: `ProductionDenseRetrievalPort::retrieve_dense` and `rebuild_and_swap` both
`.clone()`d and `.checkout()`/`.checkout_latest()`d the *same* long-lived `lancedb::Table` handle,
and `lancedb::Table::clone()` shares one mutable `pinned_version` cell — so concurrent checkouts
raced. Gap-closure plan `06.1-04` claims to fix this by giving both call sites a `DatabaseManager`
handle and opening an independently-constructed `Table` per call via `database.nodes_table()`, and
by routing `checkout_latest` failures through the existing degraded-snapshot path. This
verification independently re-reads the fixed code, re-runs the specific tests, and re-derives the
truth status for every one of the 7 roadmap success criteria — it does not take the SUMMARY, the
code review, or the orchestrator's secondhand test-run report as evidence on their own.
