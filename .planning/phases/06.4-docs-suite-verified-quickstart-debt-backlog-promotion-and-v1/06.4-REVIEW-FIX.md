### WR-07: Nothing tests that the drawn SVG matches the JSON rows; the committed-asset test is circular

**Files modified:** `eval/tests/test_forest_plot.py`
**Commit:** 4ae6ce7a
**Status:** fixed

**Applied fix:** A new test, `test_d218_each_drawn_cell_matches_its_json_row`, checks all 16 plotted cells against their `eval-forest.json` rows. It covers what the review's suggested snippet missed:
- **Value text.** The printed text must exactly equal the signed four-decimal delta, the CI bounds in order and the exact suffix (` Holm`, ` descriptive` or none). The four-decimal formatting is implemented independently in the test, with `-0.0000` normalised to `0.0000`. This catches a dropped sign, swapped bounds, or a suffix on the wrong row.
- **x position.** Each panel's value-to-x line is fitted from that panel's own drawn tick labels. The zero tick must sit on the `zero-line`.
  - The whisker ends and the point must lie on that line, within 0.02 units.
  - The whisker must lie inside the panel's `axis-line`.
  - The point must lie inside its whisker and on the correct side of zero.
  - This catches a swapped panel offset, a sign flip or a wrong scale in `x_of`.
- **Marking.** Descriptive rows must be drawn as polygons and the rest as circles. A filled point must coincide exactly with a `holm-significant` row.
- **Teeth.** A scratch mutation probe ran against the committed SVG. It mutated six things in turn: swapped CI text, a dropped sign, a "Holm" suffix on the wrong row, a moved point, a filled descriptive point and a moved whisker end. All six made the test fail, and the unmutated SVG passed. The probe was a scratch script, not committed.

The module docstring now lists this check. The generator and the committed assets are unchanged by this commit.
