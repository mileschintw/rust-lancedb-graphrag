<objective>
Add the judged half of the metric split: groundedness and faithfulness only, from a pinned judge model deliberately different from the generator, at temperature zero, with every verdict cached in a plain-text file a reviewer can open and read.

Purpose: a judged score is the least trustworthy number in the report, and the only thing that makes it publishable is the accounting around it — the judged sample size, the judge-error count, the judge model, the prompt version and an observed agreement against a hand-scored slice. Without those, a reader cannot tell a mean over one hundred items from a mean over ninety-four with six failures, and a small free-tier judge's scores carry less signal than their decimal places imply.
Output: the judge client with its cache and bounded re-ask, the two judged dimensions wired behind `score --sample N`, the calibration worksheet and agreement reporting, and the test that proves no artifact leaks the API key.
</objective>

**As a** Lancet maintainer, **I want to** get groundedness and faithfulness scores with the judge's model, prompt version, error count and human agreement printed beside them, **so that** a reader knows exactly how much to discount the number.
