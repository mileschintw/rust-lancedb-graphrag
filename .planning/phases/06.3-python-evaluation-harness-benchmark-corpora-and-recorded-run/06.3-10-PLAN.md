## Why this plan is `autonomous: false`

Not because the commands are hard to type — every one of them is automatable, and that is exactly the trap. This plan is gated on three things an agent cannot supply:

1. **The operator's own OpenRouter quota.** The seed spends embedding calls; the drive spends roughly 1,000 generation calls on the engine's key; the judged slice spends roughly 100 more on the harness's. Against a 50-per-day free-tier cap that is a multi-week activity, and even at 1,000/day a full two-armed drive consumes the entire daily allowance and starves the judge (`06.3-AI-SPEC.md` §4b, §5).
2. **A live stack.** Gateway, engine and PostgreSQL, all pointed at the isolated eval store, plus a deliberate mid-flight service stop for the preflight negative check.
3. **A human's judgment.** Twenty calibration rows hand-scored against shown evidence, and a ten-item reference-set spot-check whose whole purpose is that a person reads scored records end to end.

Plan `06.3-07` declared `false` for this same task and `06.3-04` declared `false` for less. Every task below is a checkpoint with a `<resume-signal>`; execution is expected to span sessions and to pause without losing the journal.
