is excluded from the retrieval denominators and scored as a genuine zero in the answer denominators,
and the counter that would expose the discrepancy is computed and thrown away.

Purpose: this is the phase's own defect class, relocated. The phase exists because infrastructure
failure was masquerading as a quality score, and the `is_usable()` boundary fix closed that at the
usable/unusable line. The identical shape survives one level in, for a record that passes
`is_usable()` because nothing hard failed but that carries no snapshot and no answer text — the
client's notices-only degraded-success branch. Retrieval silently drops it; exact match and F1 score
it 0.0. On the committed 2026-09-03 journal this is live, not theoretical: the primary arm holds 18
usable records with gold, 2 of them payload-less, and the report publishes
`retrieval_evidence_coverage` over 16 next to `answer_exact_match` over 18 while its own methodology
caveat promises a reader those denominators agree.

Output: one predicate, applied at both places a record enters a quality mean — the aggregate scoring
loop and the paired ablation score functions — with the excluded count published on every affected
dimension row and the report caveat rewritten to state the rule the code actually implements.

**Two documents use the identifier CR-01 for unrelated findings.** `06.3-REVIEW.md`'s CR-01 is the

phase's own review, written against the phase's final code state — is the denominator asymmetry this
plan closes. They are not the same finding and the first being closed says nothing about the second.

**The policy choice `06.3.2-VERIFICATION.md` left open, and the evidence behind it.** The gap permits
either symmetric exclusion or symmetric zero-scoring. This plan chooses symmetric exclusion at the
record level, for three reasons. It is what D-34 already says one level up — infrastructure-failed
records leave every quality mean and are reported separately with an explicit count — so the rule
becomes one rule applied consistently rather than two rules meeting at a boundary. Scoring the
retrieval side as an honest zero would require inventing a precision@k convention for zero retrieved
chunks, where the denominator is zero and no convention exists. And the population the record-level
rule would over-exclude is empty in practice: on the committed 2026-09-03 journal, across both arms,
zero usable records carry a real answer with an absent snapshot and zero carry a snapshot with a
blank answer. Every payload-less record lacks both. The record-level rule therefore discards no real
data on the evidence this repository holds, and it makes the two denominators equal unconditionally
rather than by a caveat the next reader has to trust.

**Numbers in this plan were measured, not estimated.** Primary arm (`graph-on`) of
`eval/runs/2026-09-03-multihop_rag/journal.jsonl`: 1000 records, 40 usable across both arms, 18
usable with gold in the primary arm, 2 payload-less, 16 scorable. Simulating the post-fix guard,
all four deterministic metrics return an ok status on all 16 — recall, precision, ranking and exact
match alike — so the corrected report reads 16 against 16. `form_pairs` yields 8 pairs and none of
them holds a payload-less record, so the paired half of the fix is latent on this journal and must
be proven by a constructed fixture rather than by the committed evidence.

**The gold-population split was measured too, because a published count is scoped by it.** In the
primary arm, all 18 usable records with gold carry non-null gold — zero unanswerable questions — and
both payload-less records are therefore in the answerable population. The graph-off arm holds one
null-gold usable record and four payload-less ones, all four answerable. So the answerable exclusion
count on the primary arm is 2, which is what the acceptance criteria assert, and the unanswerable
population there is empty. That empty population is itself the argument for scoping the published
count per population rather than globally: a single global figure would print an exclusion count of 2
on the `abstention_on_unanswerable` row beside a population of zero, a number a reader could not
reconcile against anything.
