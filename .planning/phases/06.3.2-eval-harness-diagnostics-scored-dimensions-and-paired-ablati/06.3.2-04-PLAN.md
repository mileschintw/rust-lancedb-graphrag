<objective>
Replace the ablation measurement that produced the phase's worst number. `graph_ablation_delta`
today averages the graph-on arm's recall scores, averages the graph-off arm's recall scores, and
subtracts — two independently scoped lists, over different questions, of different sizes. On
2026-09-03 that was 16 graph-on means against 18 graph-off means sharing only 8 questions: a
1.6% overlap reported as `0.010`.

Purpose: a paired comparison answers the question the phase is actually asking. Both arms of a
pair ran on the same question, a median of one position apart in the interleaved drive, so they
experienced near-identical system state and the difference is robust to the latency decay that
destroyed the run. Unpaired questions are dropped rather than averaged in, and the coverage is
printed beside the delta so low coverage visibly discredits the number instead of hiding inside
it.

Output: `graph_ablation_delta` redefined in place with a bootstrap interval and stratification,
four additional paired deterministic deltas, and the two cost deltas that turn "does the graph
help" into "what does the graph cost for what it gives".
</objective>
