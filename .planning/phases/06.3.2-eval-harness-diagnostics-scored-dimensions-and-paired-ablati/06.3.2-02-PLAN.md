<objective>
Turn the diagnostics plan 06.3.2-01 started capturing into the per-path and graph-health
dimensions the report has never had, and redefine `wire_contract_conformance` so it counts a
response contradicting itself rather than counting rows that parsed.

Purpose: the 2026-09-03 report showed `graph_ablation_delta = 0.010` and
`wire_contract_conformance = 1.000` on a run where 904 of 1000 queries had a hard
`RetrieveHybrid` failure. A near-zero ablation delta cannot be read at all without knowing
whether the baseline arm was alive, and a conformance score of 1.000 on a fully dead run is the
dimension that most directly licensed publishing the rest.

Output: `bm25_yield`, `retrieve_latency_ms`, `graph_presence_rate`, `graph_influence_rate`,
`graph_latency_ms` as new scored dimensions, and `wire_contract_conformance` redefined in place.
</objective>
