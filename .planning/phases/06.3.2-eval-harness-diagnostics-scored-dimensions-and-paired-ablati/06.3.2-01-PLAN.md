writing `success` on a workflow that failed. One wire field is carried the whole way — SSE
frame to journal record to usable-record filter to scored dimension to a row in `report.md` —
and the denominator convention that let infrastructure failure masquerade as a quality score
is made consistent across retrieval and answer dimensions.

from the path this plan proves end to end.

of them carried a hard node failure and an empty answer (verified directly against
`eval/runs/2026-09-03-multihop_rag/journal.jsonl`: 904 `RetrieveHybrid`, 47 `AssemblePrompt`,
9 `GenerateAnswer` hard failures; 966 empty answers; 34 snapshots). The false green came
entirely from the harness — `run.py:58` hardcodes the outcome and discards the
`QueryOutcome.status` that `client.py:232-242` already computes correctly. Until that is
fixed nothing downstream in this phase can be believed.

Output: wire-diagnostic capture (D-01), the D-27 harness-half outcome fix, the D-34 usable
record predicate and denominator convention, `unusable_record_rate` and `vector_yield` as
scored dimensions, and a report template that shows a failed dimension's diagnostic counts.
