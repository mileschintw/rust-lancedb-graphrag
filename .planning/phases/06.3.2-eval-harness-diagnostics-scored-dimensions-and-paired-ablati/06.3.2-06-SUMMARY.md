# Phase 06.3.2 Plan 06: Scorable Payload Denominator Exclusion and Paired Ablation Alignment Summary

## Implementation Overview
Closed the denominator asymmetry gap identified in `06.3.2-VERIFICATION.md` and `06.3.2-REVIEW.md` (CR-01). Previously, usable records carrying no scorable payload (e.g. notices-only success branch with empty answer and absent snapshot) were excluded from retrieval denominators while being scored as hard zeroes in answer metrics. Furthermore, the counter computed in `score.py` (`retrieval_skipped`) was never published in any report or dimension.

### Key Accomplishments:
1. **`has_scorable_payload` Predicate (`eval/src/lancet_eval/usability.py`)**:
   - Declared beside `is_usable` in the D-34 family.
   - Evaluates whether a record carries a retrieval snapshot object and non-blank stripped answer text.
   - Preserves the documented NO_EVIDENCE carve-out: a present snapshot with an empty retrieved-chunk list remains scorable and yields an honest 0.0 recall.
   - Treats `None`, empty string, and whitespace-only answers identically as lacking answer payload.

2. **Scoring Loop & Retired Dead Counter (`eval/src/lancet_eval/score.py`)**:
   - Replaced dead `retrieval_skipped` counter and its vacuous `snapshot is None` branch with an early exit in the per-arm scoring loop using `has_scorable_payload`.
   - Partitioned payload exclusions into two population-scoped accumulators: `payload_excluded_answerable` and `payload_excluded_unanswerable`.
   - The pre-existing `ndcgs` accumulator in `score.py` predated this phase, has no consumer dimension, and is outside the gap's scope; it is now also cleanly protected by the early exit.

3. **Published Detail on Affected Dimensions (`eval/src/lancet_eval/score.py`)**:
   - Extended `_build_mean_dim` to support `extra_detail` merged across ok, skipped, and error branches.
   - Published `excluded_payload_records` on all 5 quality dimensions (`retrieval_evidence_coverage`, `context_precision_at_k`, `ranking_quality`, `answer_exact_match`, `answer_f1`).
   - Published `excluded_payload_records` on `abstention_on_unanswerable` using the null-gold population count across both ok and skipped branches.

4. **Paired Ablation Score Function Alignment (`eval/src/lancet_eval/score.py`)**:
   - Applied `has_scorable_payload` to all 5 paired quality score functions: `_score_evidence_coverage`, `_score_em`, `_score_f1`, `_score_cp`, and `_score_mrr`.
   - A pair with a payload-less record in either arm is excluded from all 5 paired deltas and incremented into `excluded_unscorable_pairs`.

5. **Updated Methodology Caveat 6 (`eval/src/lancet_eval/templates/report.md.j2`)**:
   - Restated caveat 6 in normative method language without numerals to explain infrastructure failure and scorable payload exclusion, published detail, single shared record population, and paired ablation rule.

6. **Historical Re-Score Confirmation (2026-09-03 Multihop-RAG Journal)**:
   - Re-scoring a copy of `eval/runs/2026-09-03-multihop_rag` outside the working tree produced:
