<objective>
Upgrade Plan 01's append-only graph-context integration (graph facts placed after chunk evidence, unconditionally) into the score-interleaved, configurably-weighted, operator-observable integration D-29/D-30/D-32 specify — the RAG-path's production-grade shape, not the tracer's proof-of-concept shape.

Purpose: an always-on feature that runs on every `/rag/query` call must not silently degrade answer quality (unranked graph facts crowding out higher-value chunk evidence) or silently degrade observability (a failing traversal looking identical to "no relevant graph context existed").
Output: score-interleaved evidence packing with a configurable `graph_weight`; a three-way `graph_augmentation` outcome tag on every query.
</objective>
