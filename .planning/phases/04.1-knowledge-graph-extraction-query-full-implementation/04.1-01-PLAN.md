## Phase Goal

**As a** Lancet engineer, **I want to** extract entities and relationships from chunks during ingestion, persist them as a knowledge graph in LanceDB, and traverse them at query time via the now-confirmed `lance-graph` integration, **so that** RAG queries are grounded with graph context alongside the existing hybrid-retrieval evidence.

This plan delivers the first working slice of that story end-to-end: one document, a handful of entities/relations, one query. Plans 02-04 expand it (concurrency, idempotency hardening, the standalone `QueryGraph` RPC, and score-weighted interleaving) without changing this slice's shape.

<objective>
Promote the Phase 4 `graph-spike` proof-of-concept into the default build, lock the one-way schema-restructure decision with the human, and prove — with one real, runnable end-to-end test — that a document's chunks can be extracted into entities/relations, persisted as new `entities`/`edges` LanceDB rows, traversed via `lance-graph`, and rendered into a distinct, non-citable "Related Entities & Relationships" section of the RAG-answer prompt (D-01 through D-28, D-31, D-32).

Purpose: catch an architectural dead end (arrow-version bridge under real writes, LanceDB schema drift, Cypher traversal against a real narrowed neighborhood, prompt-injection boundary discipline for LLM-extracted text) after one task, not after ten already-committed layers.
Output: `entities` table live in the default schema; a working `ExtractionGenerator` seam; `ContextAssemblyStrategy` (RAG-05); graph context flowing into `/rag/query` answers.
</objective>
