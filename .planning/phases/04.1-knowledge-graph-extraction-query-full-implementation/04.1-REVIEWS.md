## Executive Summary & Overall Risk Assessment

**Overall Risk Rating: LOW**

The Phase 04.1 plan architecture provides a comprehensive, TDD-driven transition of Lancet's knowledge graph capability from a feature-gated spike (`graph-spike`) into a production-grade RAG and GraphRAG engine.

- **Foundational Execution (Plans 01–05)**: Verified clean. Dedicated LanceDB tables (`entities`, `entity_edges` in [`engine/src/db/mod.rs:231-256`](../../../engine/src/db/mod.rs#L231-L256)) ensure strict structural separation from chunk-sequence edges. Arrow IPC streaming (`decode_all_batches` in [`engine/src/graph/bridge.rs:10-72`](../../../engine/src/graph/bridge.rs#L10-L72)) prevents multi-batch truncation. Re-ingestion safety employs a two-phase transactional split with version rollback ([`engine/src/main.rs:2681-2859`](../../../engine/src/main.rs#L2681-L2859)).
- **Gap Closure (Plans 06–08)**: Plans 06, 07, and 08 directly target critical edge-case defects in the executed code:
  1. **Cypher Narrowing & Bidirectionality (Plan 06, Task 1)**: Eliminates the empty-result fallback in `narrow_via_cypher` ([`engine/src/graph/mod.rs:624-627`](../../../engine/src/graph/mod.rs#L624-L627)) and fixes `cypher_confirmed_neighbor_ids` to union forward and reverse directed Cypher traversals (resolving `lance-graph 0.5.4`'s strictly directed relationship mapping).
  2. **Edge Identity Deduplication (Plan 06, Task 2)**: Re-keys `dedup_edges_by_identity` ([`engine/src/graph/mod.rs:505-515`](../../../engine/src/graph/mod.rs#L505-L515)) onto `edge_id` instead of a 4-tuple, preventing multi-document identical relation collapse.
  3. **Frontier Cap Relocation (Plan 06, Task 3)**: Moves the `MAX_FRONTIER_SIZE` check inside the BFS hop loop to bound the final hop's frontier size before constructing SQL `IN (...)` predicates.
  4. **Queue & Orientation Verification (Plan 07)**: Adds end-to-end worker queue (`spawn_worker_with_boundary`) and target-as-seed relationship orientation tests.
  5. **Exact-Score Tie-Breaker & Diagnostics (Plan 08)**: Fixes an inverted `is_graph_b.cmp(&is_graph_a)` comparator in [`engine/src/prompt.rs:367`](../../../engine/src/prompt.rs#L367) so evidence wins exact-score ties, and adds numeric `tracing::debug!` logging to extraction validation.

The plan suite is logically sequenced, heavily tested, and fully aligned with project goals.

### Plan 04.1-06: Gap Closure — Cypher Narrowing, Edge Dedup & Frontier Caps (Wave 6 — Unexecuted)

#### Summary
Addresses three core correctness issues:
1. Fixes Cypher narrowing by removing the zero-neighbor fallback and unioning forward and reverse Cypher queries (`reverse_edge_endpoints`) to account for `lance-graph`'s strictly directed relationship mapping.
2. Re-keys `dedup_edges_by_identity` to use `edge_id` instead of a 4-tuple (`source`, `target`, `relation_type`, `weight`).
3. Relocates the `MAX_FRONTIER_SIZE` check inside `fetch_neighborhood`'s hop loop to bound the final hop's frontier.

#### Strengths
- **Thorough Directivity Fix**: Uncovered via full-suite regression testing; unioning forward and reversed directed traversals ensures bidirectional BFS compatibility without breaking `query_graph_bidirectional_seed_as_target`.
- **Edge Identity Protection (CR-01)**: Keying on `edge_id` ensures distinct extractions from separate documents sharing the same relation and confidence score are preserved.
- **Accurate Frontier Cap Placement (WR-01)**: Bounding `next_frontier.len()` immediately after extraction prevents oversized SQL `IN (...)` predicate generation on the terminal hop.

#### Concerns
- **MEDIUM — Residual Direction-Changing Multi-Hop Path Limitation**: As documented in the plan's threat model (T-04.1-30), multi-hop paths that change direction mid-chain (e.g., `seed -> A <- B` at `hop_cap > 1`) will not be matched by either forward or reverse queries alone. Acceptable because primary RAG augmentation uses 1-hop traversals where direction shifts cannot occur.

#### Suggestions
- Ensure `reverse_edge_endpoints` cleanly propagates errors on schema mismatches using `GraphSpikeErrorKind::Bridge`.

#### Risk Assessment
**LOW**: Crucial correctness fixes supported by exhaustive TDD requirements.
